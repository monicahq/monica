title: Last in touch shows when you last saw somebody
type: fix
area: Contacts
published_at: 2026-11-20T13:14:28Z
---
**Last in touch** on a contact page used to say "never" for everyone, whatever you recorded. It now shows the day of the latest life event you recorded with that person, such as a call, a meal or a meeting. News about them, such as a move or a new job, does not count. Each life event type has a switch, **Updates “Last in touch”**, so you decide which ones do. The **Last spoke** column of your contact list shows the same date, and your existing life events are already counted.
