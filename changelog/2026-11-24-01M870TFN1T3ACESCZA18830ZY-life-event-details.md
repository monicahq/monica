Until now, a life event on the vault timeline showed only its title, its date and who was there. Now each one has a **Details** button that opens everything you wrote down: the description, the time and how long it lasted, the place, the fields of its type, the attached files, and the part each person played. The details load only when you ask for them, so the timeline stays just as fast.
