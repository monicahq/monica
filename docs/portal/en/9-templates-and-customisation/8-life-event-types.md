# Add your own life event types

A life event type names the kind of thing that happened, such as a move, a new job or a meal. Monica starts you with 40, in 8 categories such as **Relationships and family** and **Leisure and culture**. Add your own for what you record often, such as **Thanksgiving dinner**, and give it the fields you want to fill in each time. @doc(life-events.record) shows how types are used.

Open your settings, and under **Personalisation** choose **Life event types**. The categories are listed on the left.

## Add a type

::::steps
:::step title="Open the form"
Choose **Add a type**.
:::

:::step title="Name it"
Pick a mark, the icon and colour it shows on the timeline. In **Name**, type `Thanksgiving dinner`, and choose **Home and daily life** in **Category**. A **Description** helps when two types could be confused.

::screenshot{label="The form of the supplied Call type, with its mark, Name, Category, and its Channel, Direction and Note fields" src="1-settings.lifeEventTypes.edit.webp"}
:::

:::step title="Add its fields"
Under **Fields**, add what you want to be asked each time, such as `Who hosted`, with **Short text** as the **Kind of answer it takes**, or `Dish you brought`, with **Dropdown**. For a choice, add its wordings under **Choices offered**. Tick **Required** when a field must always be filled in. Group fields with **Add a group** when there are many.
:::

:::step title="Add it"
Choose **Add the type**.
:::
::::

Monica says **The type is added**. It is offered the next time you record a life event.

## Change a type

Choose **Edit** on a type to change its name, category, mark or fields. You can change the supplied types too.

Changing its fields never changes the life events already recorded: they keep the fields they were written with. Only life events you record from then on ask the new questions.

## Stop offering a type, or delete it

- **Stop offering it.** On the type, switch it to **Not offered when recording**. It leaves the picker, and the life events recorded with it keep their type, their mark and their fields.
- **Delete it.** Choose **Delete this type**. This works only for a type you added that no life event uses. Monica refuses the others: **The type came with your account and stays**, or **Life events were recorded with this type, so it stays**.

## Categories

Choose **Add a category** to add one, such as `Music and choir`. A category only organises the list and the picker: it never changes what a type does.

- **Switch off** a category to stop offering all its types at once. The life events already recorded keep their type. **Switch back on** offers them again.
- A category you added can be deleted once it holds no type. The categories that came with your account cannot be deleted, only switched off.
