# Sharing, export and sync

Three ways to take what you recorded out of Monica, each for a different need:

- **A sharing link** lets one person read one contact’s page, in their browser, without an account. You choose what it shows, and you can stop it at any time. Use it when somebody needs Joey’s details for a surprise party.
- **A vCard file** is a copy of one contact that any address book can import. Use it to hand a contact over once.
- **Sync** keeps the address book and calendar on your phone or computer in step with a vault, all the time, in both directions for contacts.

None of these is collaboration. Whoever opens a sharing link can read and nothing else, and a file or a synced phone is yours. Nobody else ever joins your account.

1. @doc(sharing.create-link). Make a private, read only link to one contact.
2. @doc(sharing.what-a-link-shows). Exactly what the person holding the link can and cannot see.
3. @doc(export.vcard). Download one contact as a file.
4. @doc(integrations.overview). Choose between phone sync, the API and an AI assistant.

To set up sync on a phone or a computer, follow [CardDAV and CalDAV](/docs/carddav).
