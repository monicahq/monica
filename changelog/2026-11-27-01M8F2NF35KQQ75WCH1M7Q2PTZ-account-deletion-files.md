title: Deleting your account now removes your files and signs you out everywhere
type: fix
area: Account settings
published_at: 2026-11-27T18:00:46Z

When you deleted your account, everything in it was deleted, but the files you had uploaded to your vaults stayed in storage, and a session still open on another device stayed on record until it expired. Now both go at the same moment as the rest of your account.
