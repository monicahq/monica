# Keep lists

A list is a table whose columns you choose. It suits things you keep adding to and want to compare at a glance. Joey Tribbiani keeps his favourite sandwich shops, with a **Name**, a **City** and a **Note** for each.

A list sits in a @doc(knowledge.folders, "folder"), like a note. Every cell holds text, and you can write **bold** and web addresses in it.

## Start a list

Choose **New list** in the **+** menu of the folder column, or **Start a list** if Knowledge is still empty. The list opens as **Untitled list**, with two columns, **Name** and **Note**, and a row ready for your first item.

Click **Edit** to give it a title, a folder and a short description saying what it is for.

## Add items

Click **Add an item**. A field appears under each column: fill in what you know, then press Enter or click **Add item**. A cell you leave blank shows a dash. An item with every cell blank is not added.

To change an item, open the menu at the end of its row and choose **Edit**. The row turns into fields, and **Save changes** keeps what you typed.

## Choose the columns

Click **Edit columns** to change the shape of the list:

- Type a new name under **New column** and click **Add the column**. It goes after the others, and every item starts blank in it.
- Change a column's name and click **Rename**.
- Use the menu of a column to move it before or after its neighbour.
- Choose **Remove** in a column's menu to remove it.

:::warning
Removing a column deletes what every item holds in that column, for good.
:::

A list always keeps at least one column, so the last one cannot be removed.

## Delete an item or a list

Choose **Delete** in an item's menu to delete that item, or click **Delete** at the top of the list to delete the whole list with its columns and items. Monica asks you to confirm both, and neither can be undone.
