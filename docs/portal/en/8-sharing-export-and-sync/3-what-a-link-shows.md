# What a sharing link shows

Everything a person holding a sharing link can see, and everything they never see. @doc(sharing.create-link) explains how to make one.

## What the page shows

| Content | Shown |
| --- | --- |
| The contact’s name | Always, with their initials. Their photo is never shown |
| The facts in the page header, such as a birthday | Only when their section is shown |
| A section of the contact’s page | Unless you untick it. Every section starts ticked |
| A tab whose sections are all hidden | Never. It does not appear as an empty tab |
| Fields marked sensitive | Only when you turn on **Fields you marked sensitive**. Off by default, even inside a section you shared |
| Your name, as the person who shared it | Always, with the words **Shared with you by** |
| The end date of the link | Always, when the link has one |

## What the page never shows

Whatever you choose, a sharing link never shows:

- files and photos attached to a field, and anything inside a repeated block;
- life events, and everything you wrote about them;
- relationships, and fields that point at other people or records;
- reminders and notifications;
- the history of changes, and anything technical.

The page is read only. The person holding the link cannot write anything, reach any other page of your account, or become an account. Notifications never reach them.

## What the person sees

- **A link with a password.** The page **This page asks for a password** comes first. A wrong password shows **That password does not open this link.** and is recorded among the link’s visits.
- **A link that works.** The contact’s page, as it stands at that moment, with the tabs you shared. A change you make on the contact shows on their next click.
- **A link that expired or was revoked.** The page **This link no longer works**, saying it may have expired or been revoked, and to ask you for a new one.
- **A link to a contact you deleted.** The same **This link no longer works** page. Deleting a contact, or the vault it is in, revokes every link to it.

## What you can see about each link

For each visit, Monica records the date and time, roughly where it came from, the browser, and whether a password was asked for or failed. Nothing else about the visitor is known, because there is no account behind the link.
