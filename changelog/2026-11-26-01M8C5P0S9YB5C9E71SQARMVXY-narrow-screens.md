title: The top bar, the vault dashboard and the list of people fit a phone better
type: improvement
area: Vault
published_at: 2026-11-26T14:55:47Z
---
Until now, on a phone or a narrow window, the top bar squeezed the path to the current screen into a few letters, the tabs of the vault dashboard piled up one under the other, and the filters of the list of people broke over several lines. Now the path to the current screen has a line of its own under the top bar, tabs and filters stay on one line and scroll sideways when they do not fit, and the search field takes the whole width. On a wide screen, the labels next to the icons of the top bar appear only when there is room for them.
