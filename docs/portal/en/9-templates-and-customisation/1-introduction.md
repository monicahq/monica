# Templates and customisation

Monica works as it is, and you can shape it to what you want to remember. Everything here lives in your settings, under **Personalisation**.

- **Templates** decide which tabs, sections and fields a contact’s page holds. Start with @doc(templates.versions), then follow @doc(templates.build-your-own).
- **Relationship types** are the words for how two people are connected, such as **Friend** or **Godparent**.
- **Life event types** are the kinds of things that happen, such as a move or a Thanksgiving dinner, and what each one asks about.
- **Profile types** are the kinds of records that are not people, such as pets.
- **Value lists** are the choices a field offers, such as the categories of contacts.
