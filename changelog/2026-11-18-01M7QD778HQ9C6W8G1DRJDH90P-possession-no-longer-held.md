title: A possession you no longer have cannot be lent or moved
type: improvement
area: Possessions
published_at: 2026-11-18T13:23:28Z
---
Once a possession is sold, given away, thrown away, lost or stolen, Monica no longer lets you move it or record a planned or active loan of it. A returned loan can still be recorded, so its history stays complete.
