Until now, the only way to reach Monica from outside the browser was to create an API key by hand and paste it somewhere. Now the Monica app for Android signs you in through your usual sign-in page, opened in your phone’s browser, on app.monicahq.com or on any Monica someone runs on their own server, with nothing for its administrator to set up. Each phone signed in appears under Mobile apps in your security settings, where you can sign one out if you lose it.
