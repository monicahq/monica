# Share a contact with a link

A sharing link is a private address to one contact’s page. Whoever opens it needs no account, can change nothing, and sees only what you chose to show. You can change what it shows, or stop it, at any time.

Say Rachel is organising Joey’s birthday and needs his address and phone number. You make her a link to Joey’s page that shows only his contact details, works for seven days, and asks for a password.

Only contacts can be shared this way. There is no link to a whole vault.

## Make the link

::::steps
:::step title="Open the sharing links"
On Joey’s page, open the menu next to **Edit contact** and choose **Share**. The screen lists the links you already made for him.
:::

:::step title="Start a new link"
Choose **Create sharing link**, or **Create your first sharing link** when there is none yet.

::screenshot{label="The Share Joey Tribbiani form, with Name this link, Who it is for, Stops working, Password and the choices of what the recipient sees" src="1-contacts.shareLinks.new.webp"}
:::

:::step title="Name it"
In **Name this link**, type `For Rachel, the birthday party`, so you can tell it apart from the others later. **Who it is for** is a note for you. Nobody is emailed.
:::

:::step title="Choose when it stops"
In **Stops working**, choose **In 7 days**. The other choices are **In 30 days**, **In 3 months** and **Never**. A link that never stops is one you have to remember to revoke.
:::

:::step title="Add a password"
Type a password in **Password**. Whoever opens the link is asked for it before the page opens. Leave it empty for a link that anybody holding the address can open.
:::

:::step title="Choose what it shows"
Under **Sections of their page**, every section starts ticked. Untick the ones Rachel does not need, and keep **Contact information** and **Addresses**. Under **What the recipient sees**, leave **Fields you marked sensitive** off unless she needs them. The summary under **Going out** counts the sections the link will show.
:::

:::step title="Create it"
Choose **Create sharing link**.
:::
::::

Monica says **The link is live** and shows the address.

:::warning
Copy the address now, with **Copy the link**. This is the only time Monica can show it: it keeps only a scrambled version, so a lost address cannot be recovered. If you did not copy it, revoke the link and make another.
:::

## Send it

Monica does not send anything. Paste the address into the message you already use with Rachel. If you set a password, send it to her separately, for example by phone, so that one intercepted message is not enough to open the page.

To see the page as Rachel will see it, choose **Open it as a visitor**.

## See who opened it

Each link keeps a list of its visits, with the date, the time and roughly where each came from. Wrong passwords appear there too. Because there is no account behind the link, nothing else about the visitor is recorded.

## Change what a link shows

On the link, choose **Change** or **Change what For Rachel, the birthday party shows**, adjust the sections, the expiry or the password, then choose **Save what it shows**. Monica says **The link is changed**. The address stays the same, and Rachel sees the change the next time she opens a page.

To remove the password, tick **Stop asking for a password**. To keep the current one, leave **Password** empty.

## Revoke a link

:::warning
Revoking a link takes effect straight away and cannot be undone. If the same person needs access again, make a new link.
:::

On the link, choose **Revoke this link**. Monica asks **Revoke “For Rachel, the birthday party”?** Choose **Revoke the link** to confirm. Monica says **The link is revoked**.

The link stays on the list, marked **Revoked**, with the visits it had, so you keep the history of who saw the page.

A link also stops working on its own when it reaches its end date, when you delete the contact, or when you delete the vault the contact is in.
