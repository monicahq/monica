# Record your first person

Add Rachel Green to your Personal vault, write down her birthday and her email address, link her to Ross Geller, and let Monica remind you of her birthday every year.

This tutorial uses Rachel Green as the example. Follow it with somebody from your own life if you prefer: the steps are the same.

## What you will learn

Every person in Monica has a page, divided into tabs, and each tab holds sections such as **Personal details** or **Contact information**. You fill a section in when you know something, and leave it empty when you do not. Along the way you will see how a date you write down becomes a reminder.

## Before you start

You need an account, and your **Personal** vault open. If you have neither yet, follow @doc(start.create-account) first.

On monicahq.com’s free plan, each person in your list of people counts toward the number of people you can keep.

## Step 1: Add Rachel

::::steps
:::step title="Open the form for a new person"
On the dashboard of your **Personal** vault, choose **Add the first person**. Once the vault holds somebody, the button reads **Add somebody**.
:::

:::step title="Enter her name"
Type `Rachel` in **First name** and `Green` in **Last name**. Every other field on this form is optional, and you can leave them empty.

::screenshot{label="The New contact form in the Personal vault, with the Name section and the Create the contact button" src="1-contacts.new.webp"}
:::

:::step title="Create the contact"
Choose **Create the contact**.
:::
::::

**Check your work.** Monica says **Rachel Green is written down** and opens Rachel’s page on its **Overview** tab. The rest of the page is empty, ready for what you know about her.

## Step 2: Write down her birthday, and ask for a reminder

A birthday is a date that comes back every year, so Monica can remind you of it. It does so only when you ask.

::::steps
:::step title="Open the General tab"
At the top of Rachel’s page, choose the **General** tab. It holds the **Personal details** section.
:::

:::step title="Edit Personal details"
In **Personal details**, choose **Edit**.
:::

:::step title="Enter the date"
In **Birthday**, enter 5 May 1969. If you know the day but not the year, enter any year and tick **I do not know the year**.
:::

:::step title="Ask for a reminder"
Tick **Remind me about this date**. Leave **Repeats** on **Every year**. You can also choose the time, the time zone, and what the reminder says in **It will say**.

::screenshot{label="Rachel’s Personal details section in edit mode, with her birthday and Remind me about this date ticked, repeating every year" src="2-contacts.sections.edit.webp"}
:::

:::step title="Save the section"
Choose **Save this section**.
:::
::::

**Check your work.** **Personal details** now shows Rachel’s birthday.

## Step 3: Add her email address

::::steps
:::step title="Open the Ways to connect tab"
Choose the **Ways to connect** tab, then choose **Edit** in **Contact information**.
:::

:::step title="Enter the address"
Under **Emails**, type `rachel.green@fortunata.example`. To add a second address later, you would choose **Add another**.
:::

:::step title="Save the section"
Choose **Save this section**.
:::
::::

**Check your work.** **Contact information** lists the address.

## Step 4: Link her to Ross

Rachel does not live in isolation. Linking her to Ross records how they know each other, and shows on both of their pages.

::::steps
:::step title="Open the Relationships tab"
Choose the **Relationships** tab, then **Add a relationship**.
:::

:::step title="Choose the relationship"
In **Relationship type**, choose **Friend**.
:::

:::step title="Name the other person"
Choose **I know their name**, then type `Ross` in **First name** and `Geller` in **Last name**. Ross does not need to be in Monica already: this form writes him down for you.
:::

:::step title="Give Ross a page of his own"
Tick **Create a contact record for this person**, so that Ross appears in your list of people like Rachel. Left off, Ross still gets a page, but stays out of that list and out of the count of the free plan.
:::

:::step title="Add the relationship"
Choose **Add relationship**.
:::
::::

**Check your work.** Monica says **The relationship is recorded** and shows Ross on Rachel’s **Relationships** tab. You do not need to add the relationship again from Ross’s side: it reads from both pages at once.

## Step 5: Find the reminder

::::steps
:::step title="Look on Rachel’s page"
Choose the **Overview** tab. The **Reminders** panel lists her birthday.
:::

:::step title="Look in the vault"
In the vault’s sidebar, choose **Reminders**. The **Coming up** tab lists Rachel’s birthday among everything Monica will remind you of in this vault.

::screenshot{label="The vault’s Reminders screen on the Coming up tab, with Reminders selected in the vault’s sidebar" src="3-reminders.index.webp"}
:::
::::

## What you did

Rachel Green now has a page with her birthday and her email address, Ross Geller is recorded as her friend, and Monica will remind you of her birthday every year.

If a step did not match what you see:

- **No Remind me about this date box.** It appears only on date fields, while the section is in edit mode. Choose **Edit** in **Personal details** first.
- **No reminder listed.** Open **Personal details** again and check that **Remind me about this date** is ticked. Saving the section with the box unticked removes the reminder, and keeps the date.
- **Ross is missing from your list of people.** **Create a contact record for this person** was left off. His page still exists, and Rachel’s **Relationships** tab leads to it.
