# Add your own relationship types

A relationship type is the word for how two people are connected. Monica starts you with ten, such as **Parent / Child**, **Sibling**, **Spouse** and **Friend**, filed under the categories **Family**, **Romantic**, **Friendship**, **Professional** and **Other**. Add your own when none of them says it, such as **Roommate** for Monica and Rachel. @doc(relationships.add) shows how types are used.

Open your settings, and under **Personalisation** choose **Relationship types**.

::screenshot{label="The Relationship types screen, with the supplied types listed under Family, Romantic, Friendship, Professional and Other" src="1-settings.relationshipTypes.index.webp"}

## Add a type

::::steps
:::step title="Open the form"
Choose **Add a type**.
:::

:::step title="Name it and file it"
In **Name**, type `Roommate`, and in **Category**, choose **Friendship**. The category decides only where the type is listed.
:::

:::step title="Say how it reads"
Under **How it reads**, choose **The same from both sides**, since Monica and Rachel are each other’s roommate, and type `Roommate` in **Role, on both sides**.

For a link that reads differently from each side, such as a godparent and a godchild, choose **Different on each side**, and fill in **First role** and **Opposite role**. The sentence below the form shows how it will read.
:::

:::step title="Add it"
Leave **Offer this type for new relationships** on, and choose **Add the type**.
:::
::::

Monica says **The type is added**. It is offered the next time you link two people.

## Change or reorder types

Choose **Edit** on a type to change its name, category or wording. Everything already written with it reads in the new words. Drag a type by its handle to change the order the form offers them in.

The direction of a type stays fixed once relationships use it, and always for a supplied type, so relationships written years ago still read the way they were written.

## Family tree and romantic history

**Parent / Child** draws the family tree, and **Partner**, **Fiancé** and **Spouse** make up romantic history. Renaming them, moving them to another category, or no longer offering them changes nothing about that. A type you add never feeds the family tree.

## Stop offering a type, or delete it

- **Stop offering it.** On the type, turn off **Offer this type for new relationships**. It leaves the relationship form, and everything already written with it keeps reading as it does.
- **Delete it.** Choose **Delete**, then **Delete the type**. This works only for a type you added that no relationship uses. Monica refuses the others: **The type came with your account and stays**, or **Relationships are written with this type, so it stays**.

## Categories

Choose **Add a category** to add one, such as `Neighbourhood`. Categories you add can be renamed, moved up and down, and deleted once they hold no type. The categories that came with your account cannot be renamed or deleted.
