Each life event on a vault’s timeline now has a **Details** button. It opens a panel under the entry with everything you wrote down: the description, the time and how long it lasted, the place, the fields of its type, the files attached to it, and who was there with the part they played. The details load only when you ask for them, so the timeline stays as fast as before.
