title: Administrators are now emailed about support requests
type: feature
area: Support
published_at: 2026-11-24T22:17:24Z
---
Until now, administrators found out about a support request only by opening the admin space. Now every administrator of the instance gets an email when somebody opens a request or writes again in one. It says whether the request is new or a follow up, names the account it came from, and links to the whole conversation.
