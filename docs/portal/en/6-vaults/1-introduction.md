# Vaults

A vault holds one part of your life. Everything you record in Monica lives in a vault: its people, their relationships, their life events and their reminders. Monica starts you with one, **Personal**, and many people never need another.

A second vault helps when two parts of your life should stay apart on screen. You might keep friends and family in **Personal**, and the people you meet through work in a vault called **Work**. Each vault has its own list of people, its own reminders and its own settings, and a search in one vault never reaches the other. A vault is a way to organise, not a lock: nobody else is in your account to keep out. @doc(concepts.model) explains the idea in full.

Choose the vault before you add somebody, because Monica cannot move a contact from one vault to another, and two people can only be linked by a relationship when they are in the same vault.

1. @doc(vaults.create). Start a second vault, and choose which vault is the default.
2. @doc(vaults.settings). Rename a vault, change its colour, and choose the template new contacts start from.
3. @doc(vaults.delete). Remove a vault and everything in it.

On monicahq.com, the free offer holds one vault, so a second one needs full access.
