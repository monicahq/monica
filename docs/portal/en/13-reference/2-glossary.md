# Words Monica uses

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | Everything you keep in Monica. It belongs to one person, you, and nobody else joins it. See @doc(concepts.model). |
| **Administrator** | Somebody who looks after an instance of Monica: its accounts, storage and settings. An administrator never sees inside your vaults. See @doc(concepts.privacy). |
| **Answers to questions no longer asked** | What you wrote in a field that the contact’s template no longer has. Kept out of sight, never deleted. Also called set aside answers. See @doc(contacts.edit). |
| **Contact** | A person you record. Each has a page of their own. |
| **Favourite** | A contact kept at the top of the list of people. See @doc(contacts.add). |
| **Field** | One question on a contact’s page, such as **Birthday** or **Email**. Sometimes called a custom field elsewhere. See @doc(reference.field-types). |
| **Free offer** | What monicahq.com holds without payment: one vault, 30 contacts and 100 MB of storage by default. |
| **Full access** | monicahq.com without the limits of the free offer. |
| **Identifying field** | A field Monica uses to spot duplicates, such as an email address or a phone number. See @doc(duplicates.review). |
| **Instance** | A copy of Monica somebody runs on their own server. monicahq.com is one instance among others. |
| **Life event** | Something that happened on a date, often with other people, such as a wedding or a move. See @doc(life-events.record). |
| **Me contact** | A contact that stands for you. No screen creates it yet. |
| **Merge** | Making two records of one person into one. It can be undone. See @doc(duplicates.merge). |
| **Page** | A tab on a contact’s page, such as **General** or **Ways to connect**. |
| **Partial contact** | Somebody written down with a name only, kept out of the list of people. See @doc(contacts.add). |
| **Profile** | A record of something that is not a person, such as a pet. See @doc(profiles.add). |
| **Profile type** | A kind of profile, such as **Animals**. Also called a kind of profile. See @doc(customise.profile-types). |
| **Relationship** | How two people are connected, such as **Friend** or **Parent / Child**. See @doc(relationships.add). |
| **Reminder** | A rule telling Monica to tell you about something, on a day and at a time. Each day it falls on is a date you answer. See @doc(reminders.create). |
| **Section** | A group of fields on a page, such as **Contact information**. |
| **Sensitive field** | A field hidden on screen until you reveal it, and left out of sharing links unless you include it. See @doc(concepts.privacy). |
| **Sharing link** | A private, read only address to one contact’s page. See @doc(sharing.create-link). |
| **Template** | The shape of a contact’s page: its pages, sections and fields, published in versions. See @doc(templates.versions). |
| **Value list** | The choices a field offers, such as the categories of contacts. See @doc(customise.value-lists). |
| **Vault** | A compartment for one part of your life, holding its own contacts and reminders. A vault is not a group of people and not a shared space. See @doc(vaults.introduction). |
| **Version** | One published state of a template. Contacts stay on their version until you move them. See @doc(templates.apply-new-version). |
