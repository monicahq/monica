title: A new contact starts on the template version the vault uses
type: fix
area: Contacts
published_at: 2026-11-18T13:44:51Z
---
When your account had several contact templates and a vault used an older version of one of them, the new contact form picked another template on its own, at its latest version. It now starts on the version the vault uses, unless you choose another template.
