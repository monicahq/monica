# Keep a record of a pet or anything else

Not everything you want to remember is a person. Ross had a monkey called Marcel, and you might want to note his species, his birthday, and who looks after him. Monica keeps records like this as profiles.

## Profiles and contacts

A profile is a record of something that is not a person. Each profile belongs to a kind of profile, and Monica prepares one kind for you: **Animals**. You can create other kinds in your account settings, under **Kinds of profile**.

A profile differs from a contact in two ways:

- Relationships connect contacts only, so a profile never appears on a contact’s **Relationships** tab. A pet is linked to its people through its own fields instead, such as **Caregivers**.
- Profiles do not count toward the free offer on monicahq.com, which counts contacts.

@doc(concepts.model) explains how profiles fit with vaults and contacts.

## Add a pet

Each kind of profile has its own entry in the vault’s sidebar, under **People**.

::::steps
:::step title="Open the list of animals"
In the vault’s sidebar, choose **Animals**, then **Add Animal**.
:::

:::step title="Enter the name"
Type `Marcel` in **Name**. It is the only required field.

::screenshot{label="The New Animal form, with the Name field, the Animal details section, and Caregivers under People" src="1-entities.new.webp"}
:::

:::step title="Fill in the details"
In **Animal details**, fill in what you know: **Species**, **Breed**, **Sex**, **Birthday**, **Adoption date** and **Microchip number**. If you know the day of a date but not the year, tick **I do not know the year**.
:::

:::step title="Name who looks after him"
Under **Caregivers**, choose **Add another**. In **Person**, pick Ross Geller from your contacts, and in **Role**, type what he is to Marcel, such as `Owner`.
:::

:::step title="Save"
Choose **Save**.
:::
::::

Monica says **Marcel is written down** and lists him under **Animals**. The form also offers **Care**, for food, routine and health notes, and **Notes**. These sections come from the **Basic animal** template.

## What you can do with a profile today

:::note
A profile has no page of its own yet. Once saved, it appears in its list, but you cannot open it, change it or delete it from the web.
:::

Write down everything you know before you choose **Save**. The information stays safe, and becomes reachable as soon as profiles get a page.

## When Add is unavailable

The list of a kind of profile says why when you cannot add to it:

- **This type is turned off.** The kind of profile is switched off in your account settings. Turn it on again under **Kinds of profile**.
- **Nothing can be added until this vault has a published template.** The vault has no published template for this kind of profile. In your account settings, check that the template, **Basic animal** for animals, is published and turned on.
