Until now, a contact stayed on the template it was created with, and nothing on its page let you move it to another one. Now the Template panel on Edit contact lets you pick another template, or another version of the one it uses. Before anything changes, Monica shows you the answers that will go to Unused answers, the ones that will come back, and the reminders that will stop or start again. Nothing you wrote is deleted, and you can switch back at any time.
