title: An unknown year is now kept on a date in a repeated field
type: fix
area: Contacts
published_at: 2026-11-25T17:55:44Z
---
Until now, when a date sat in a field you can repeat, such as a list of anniversaries, ticking "I do not know the year" had no effect, and the date was saved with a year. Now the year is left out, as it is for any other date.
