Every account now has four more templates beside Basic contact: Essentials, Friends and family, Professional network and Full portrait. Pick one when you add a contact, or move a contact to one at any time. Basic contact stays the template a new contact starts from.

When a contact moves from one template to another, what both templates have in common, such as a birthday, emails or phone numbers, comes along with it.
