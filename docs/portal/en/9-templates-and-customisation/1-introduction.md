# Templates and customisation

Monica works as it is, and you can shape it to what you want to remember. Everything here lives in your settings, under **Personalisation**.

- **Templates** decide which tabs, sections and fields a contact’s page holds. Start with @doc(templates.versions), then follow @doc(templates.build-your-own).
- **Relationship types** are the words for how two people are connected, such as **Friend** or **Godparent**.
- **Life event types** are the kinds of things that happen, such as a move or a Thanksgiving dinner, and what each one asks about.
- **Profile types** are the kinds of records that are not people, such as pets.
- **Value lists** are the choices a field offers, such as the categories of contacts.

Every account starts with these templates for contacts. **Basic contact** is the one a new contact starts from, and you can pick any of the others when you add somebody.

| Template | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Basic contact** | The details most people note down: categories, birthday, ways to reach somebody, documents and photos. |
| **Essentials** | Keep your contacts’ details and essential landmarks at hand every day. |
| **Friends and family** | Remember the small things that matter, to take care of your relationships. |
| **Professional network** | Remember people, their work and the common ground that feeds the relationship. |
| **Full portrait** | Build a rich record over time. |

A contact can move to another template at any time. What both templates have in common, such as a birthday or an email address, comes along with it. What the new template has no place for is kept aside, and comes back if the contact returns to a template that has it. @doc(contacts.change-template) shows how.

Every account also starts with two templates for animals. **Basic animal** is the one a new animal starts from, and you can pick **Animal** instead when you add one.

| Template | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Basic animal** | The essentials on one page: who the animal is, who looks after it, its care and a note. |
| **Animal** | Remember the animals that matter, their habits and the people who look after them. |
