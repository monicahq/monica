# The template marketplace

The marketplace is a public catalogue of templates and sections that people built for themselves: a profile for a dog, a template for a therapist’s clients, a section for a band’s contacts. The idea is simple: somebody has usually built the thing you need already.

## What you can do today

You can browse the catalogue. Choose **Marketplace** at the top of any screen, or open it without an account. Search by name, and narrow the list by kind (**Templates** or **Sections**), by category and by language, sorted by **Recently updated**, **Recently published**, **Most installed** or **Name**.

::screenshot{label="The Marketplace screen, with its figures, the search box, the filters by kind and the list of categories" src="1-marketplace.index.webp"}

The catalogue holds only what an administrator of the instance approved. On a new instance, it may be empty.

## What is not open yet

- **Installing.** The catalogue shows each listing, but there is no way yet to install one into your account.
- **Publishing.** The share screen of a template says **Writing a listing is not open yet**: publishing your own work into the catalogue is the next thing being built.

Until then, share a template with one person as a file, which works today. @doc(templates.import-export) shows how.

## How installing is meant to work

The marketplace is designed so that installing makes a private copy in your account, which you can change however you want. The author never sees your copy or your contacts. When the author posts a new version, your copy does not change on its own: moving to it is always your choice, the same way contacts move to a new template version only when you decide, as @doc(templates.versions) explains.

This describes the design, not something you can do yet.
