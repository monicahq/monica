# Change your name, email address and photo

Open your settings and choose **Profile**.

::screenshot{label="The Profile screen, with Profile photo, Details, Email address and Delete your account" src="1-settings.profile.index.webp"}

## Your name

Under **Details**, change **First name** or **Last name**, and choose **Save changes**. Monica says **Your details are saved.** Your name appears wherever Monica shows who did something, such as your logs.

## Your photo

Under **Profile photo**, choose a file and **Upload**. Monica takes JPEG, PNG and WebP images up to 5 MB, and a square photo reads best. Without one, Monica shows your initials. To take it off, choose **Remove the photo**, then confirm.

## Your email address

The address you sign in with is also where every way back into the account is sent: sign in links, password resets and warnings. Change it with care.

::::steps
:::step title="Type the new address"
Under **Email address**, type the new one, and choose **Save this address**. Monica asks you to confirm it is you first.
:::

:::step title="Confirm it"
Monica sends an email to the new address, with the subject **Confirm your email address**. Open it and choose **Confirm my email address**. The link works for an hour.
:::
::::

Until you confirm the new address, Monica shows the confirmation screen instead of your account. If the email did not arrive, choose **Send the email again** there.

The email reads as if you had just signed up, because it is the same one Monica sends to new accounts. You can follow it all the same.
