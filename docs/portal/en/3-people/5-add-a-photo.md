# Add a photo to a contact

Give a contact a photo, so you recognise them at a glance in the list of people and on their page. Without one, Monica draws their initials on a coloured background.

## Upload a photo

::::steps
:::step title="Open Edit contact"
On the contact’s page, choose **Edit contact**.
:::

:::step title="Choose the photo"
In **Photo**, choose the file on your device. Monica accepts JPEG, PNG and WebP images up to 5 MB. A square photo reads best, because Monica shows it in a circle.

::screenshot{label="The Photo panel of Edit contact for Chandler Bing, with his initials, the file chooser and the Upload button" src="1-contacts.edit.webp"}
:::

:::step title="Upload it"
Choose **Upload**.
:::
::::

Monica says **The photo is saved**, and shows it wherever the contact appears. To change it, upload another one: the new photo replaces the old.

## Remove a photo

In **Photo**, choose **Remove the photo**, then confirm. The contact is drawn with their initials again.

## Photos and storage

On monicahq.com, a photo counts toward the storage your account is allowed. If an upload would take the account past it, Monica refuses the photo and says **This file would take the account past the storage it is allowed.** On an instance somebody runs on their own server, there is no storage limit.

Your own photo, the one shown next to your name, is set in your account settings, not here.
