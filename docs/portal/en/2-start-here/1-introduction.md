# Start here

Three pages take you from nothing to a Monica you can use every day. Read them in order the first time.

1. @doc(start.create-account). Sign up, confirm your email address, and open the vault Monica prepares for you.
2. @doc(start.first-contact). Add Rachel Green, fill in her birthday and her email address, link her to Ross Geller, and watch her birthday reminder appear.
3. @doc(concepts.model). Understand where everything you record lives, and what belongs to what.

At the end you will have an account, a first person with a yearly birthday reminder, and a clear picture of how Monica is organised.

If you already have an account, start with @doc(start.first-contact).
