# Describe the things you own

A possession type is a kind of thing you own, such as **Books**, **Cars** or **Musical instruments**. It says which details a thing of that kind carries: an author, a registration number, a tuning. Types belong to your account, so every vault sees the same ones.

Open your settings, and under **Possessions** choose **Possession types**.

## The types you start with

Your account comes with 25 types already written, each with fields that suit it and an icon of its kind: **Books**, **Comics**, **Vinyl Records**, **Coins**, **Wine**, **Cars**, **Bicycles**, **Household appliances**, **Electronics and computers**, **Furniture**, **Jewellery**, **Musical instruments**, **Homes** and more. **Other** has no field at all, for whatever fits nowhere else.

They are written in the language you chose when you signed up. They are yours like any type you create: rename them, change their fields, or delete the ones you do not need. A type you delete does not come back.

A type describes a kind of thing. Where the things are kept is the separate list of [locations](locations), and the things themselves are your @doc(customise.possessions, "possessions").

## Add a type

::::steps
:::step title="Create it"
Choose **New type**. A type called **Untitled type** opens.
:::

:::step title="Name it"
In **Name**, type `Office equipment`, then leave the field. Pick a colour under **Colour** and an icon under **Icon**. A type with no icon shows a blank page.
:::

:::step title="Add its fields"
Choose **Add a field**. A text field appears; name it `Serial number`. Choose **Add a field** again for `Condition`, set its **Kind** to **Choice from a list**, and add the choices `New`, `Used` and `Broken`.
:::
::::

Every change is saved as soon as you leave a field. There is no button to press.

## Group fields

When a type has many fields, gather them under a name. Choose **Add a group**, name it `Purchase`, then use the plus button on the group to add `Price` and `Shop` inside it.

Move a group or a field with its up and down buttons. A field moves among the fields of its own group, or among the standalone fields.

Removing a group keeps its fields. They become standalone fields, after the ones already there.

## Kinds of field

| Kind | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| Text | A few words, such as a serial number |
| Number | A quantity, such as a power rating |
| Date | A day, such as the end of a warranty |
| Yes or no | One of two answers |
| Choice from a list | One of the choices you wrote |
| Rating from one to five | A score |

## Share or back up a type

Choose **Export as JSON** on a type to see its file. Copy it or download it. The file holds the name, the colour, the icon, the groups and the fields, and nothing you wrote about a thing you own.

To bring a file in, choose **Import JSON** on the list of types, paste the file and choose **Check this file**. Monica lists every problem it finds before anything is created. A file that can be imported arrives as a new type, and the types you already have stay as they are.

## Delete a type

At the bottom of a type, choose **Delete this type**. Its groups and fields go with it, and so does every possession of that type, with the details it held. This cannot be undone.
