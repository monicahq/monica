Until now, your favourite and recently viewed people sat above the timeline on the dashboard of a vault. Now the dashboard opens on one row of tabs: Timeline, Needs your attention, Favourite people and Recently viewed. Each list gets the full width of the page, and each tab shows how many it holds.
