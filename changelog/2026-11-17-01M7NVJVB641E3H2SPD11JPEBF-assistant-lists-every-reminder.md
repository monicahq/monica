Asked for the reminders of a vault, an assistant connected to Monica could answer that there were none, because it only looked at the next seven days. It can now list every reminder, whatever its date, and knows that the coming days are a window, not the whole list.

The descriptions an assistant reads are clearer too: one word for one idea, the accepted values of every choice, and where to find each id it needs.
