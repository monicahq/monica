# Disconnect an assistant

An AI assistant, such as Claude or ChatGPT, can connect to Monica by asking you to sign in and approve it, rather than with an API key. Once approved, it acts as you until you disconnect it. The [MCP guide](/docs/mcp) explains how to connect one.

## See connected assistants

Open your settings and choose **Security and access**. **Connected assistants** lists each assistant you approved, with when it was connected. When there is none, it says **No assistant connected**.

An assistant that uses an API key instead is listed under **API keys**. See @doc(security.api-keys).

## Disconnect one

::::steps
:::step title="Start"
Choose **Disconnect** on the assistant.
:::

:::step title="Confirm"
Monica says it loses access at once and has to ask you again to come back. Choose **Disconnect**. Monica asks you to confirm it is you first.
:::
::::

Monica says **The assistant is disconnected.** It can no longer reach your account, and has to ask you again before it can.

## When an assistant reports a problem

When a tool of Monica fails, is missing or misleads an assistant, the assistant can send a report to the administrators of your Monica, so they can fix the tool. It is asked to describe your request in its own words and to leave out names, dates and other details from your vaults. The report says which account it came from.

Each report appears in your account log, and the call appears in **MCP history**, with what the assistant sent.
