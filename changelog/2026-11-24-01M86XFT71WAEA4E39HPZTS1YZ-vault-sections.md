Until now, every vault showed People, Things and Knowledge in its sidebar, even the ones you never use. Now you can switch any of them off in Vault settings, under **What this vault shows**. The section leaves the sidebar, and People leaves the search too. Nothing is deleted: every record still opens from a link, and the API and connected assistants still reach all of it, and can change the setting as well.
