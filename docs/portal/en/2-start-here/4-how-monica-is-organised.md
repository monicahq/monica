# How Monica is organised

Where everything you record lives, and what belongs to what.

You do not need this page to use Monica. Read it when you want to know why things are arranged the way they are, or before you set up a second vault.

## The whole picture

Everything you record sits inside one account, like this:

- **Your account**, which belongs to you alone.
  - **Vaults**, one or more. Monica starts you with **Personal**.
    - **Contacts**: the people you record, such as Rachel Green and Ross Geller.
    - **Profiles**: records of things that are not people, such as a pet.
- **Templates**, which give every contact and every profile its tabs, sections and fields.

The sections below take each of these in turn.

## Your account is yours alone

An account holds exactly one person. There are no members, no invitations and no roles: nobody else signs in to your account, and nobody else sees your vaults. If somebody else wants to use Monica, they create an account of their own.

Monica is a record of *your* view of the people around you. Rachel Green appears in your account because you wrote her down. She is not told, and she has no access to what you wrote.

The same is true of whoever runs the Monica you use. An instance has an administrator, who looks after the instance itself: its accounts, its storage and its settings. The administrator’s screens never show what is inside your vaults.

## Vaults are compartments

A vault groups the people of one part of your life. You might keep your friends and family in **Personal**, and the people you meet through work in a second vault.

A vault is a way to organise, not a lock. It does not keep anybody out, because nobody else is in your account in the first place. Use a second vault when you want two parts of your life kept apart on screen: each vault has its own list of people, its own reminders and its own settings.

Every contact lives in exactly one vault. Monica has no way yet to move a contact from one vault to another, so choose the vault before you add somebody.

## Contacts and profiles

A **contact** is a person. Each contact has a page of their own, and the page is where everything you know about them goes: their birthday, their email address, how they are connected to others, and what happened to them.

A **profile** is a record of something that is not a person, such as a pet. Monica prepares one kind of profile for you, **Animals**, and you can create other kinds, for instance for companies or places you want to remember.

The difference matters for relationships. Ross can be Rachel’s **friend**, because both are people. Relationships connect contacts only, so a profile never appears on a contact’s **Relationships** tab.

## Templates shape every page

A template decides what a page holds: which tabs it has, which sections each tab shows, and which fields each section offers. Monica starts you with two, **Basic contact** for people and **Basic animal** for pets.

When you opened Rachel’s page in @doc(start.first-contact), its **General** and **Ways to connect** tabs came from **Basic contact**. The **Overview** and **Relationships** tabs are always there, whatever the template.

You can change a template, or build your own, to hold what matters to you. A change never alters the pages that already use the template until you choose to apply it. Monica keeps each published version of a template as it was, and every contact stays on its version until you move it to a newer one. This is what lets you reshape your templates without ever losing, or silently rearranging, what you already wrote.

## What stays fixed, and what you can change

Some ideas in Monica are fixed, because the rest of the product relies on them: the account, the vault, the contact, the relationship, the life event and the reminder. A contact, for example, always has a name made of its usual parts.

Everything else is yours to shape through templates. A birthday or a gender is not built in: they are ordinary fields that **Basic contact** happens to supply, and you can remove them or add fields of your own.

## The contact that stands for you

Monica has the idea of a contact that represents you, called the Me contact. No screen creates it yet, so you will not see it in your vault today.
