# Choose a new password when you forgot it

Ask Monica for a link by email, and use it to choose a new password.

If you only want to get in, a link that signs you in is faster: see @doc(signin.magic-link).

::::steps
:::step title="Ask for a link"
On the sign in screen, choose **Forgot your password?** Type your **Email address**, then choose **Email me a link**.
:::

:::step title="Open the email"
Monica says a link is on its way if the address has an account. Open the email with the subject **Choose a new password for Monica**, and choose **Choose a new password**. The link works for an hour.
:::

:::step title="Choose the new password"
Type it in **New password** and again in **Confirm password**. It needs at least 8 characters. Choose **Change my password**.
:::

:::step title="Sign in"
Monica says **Your password is changed. You can sign in with it now.** Sign in with the new password.
:::
::::

Monica then emails you with the subject **Your Monica password was changed**, so you would notice if somebody else did it.

## What to know

- **The message is the same for everybody.** Monica confirms that a link is on its way whether or not the address has an account, so nobody can use this screen to find out who is registered.
- **One link a minute.** Asking again straight away sends nothing new. Wait a minute.
- **An old link stops working.** If you see **That link no longer works. Ask for another one.**, the hour has passed or you already used it.
- **You stay signed in elsewhere.** Changing your password does not sign you out on your other devices.

If no email arrives, look in your spam folder, then ask again.
