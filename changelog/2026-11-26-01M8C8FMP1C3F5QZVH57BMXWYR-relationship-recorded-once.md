title: The same relationship can no longer be recorded twice
type: fix
area: Contacts
published_at: 2026-11-26T15:44:44Z
---
Until now, Monica let you link two people the same way twice, for instance marking Jim as Pam’s friend and then Pam as Jim’s friend, and both contact pages then listed the friendship twice. Now Monica refuses the second link and says the two people are already linked this way, whether you add it on a contact page, through the API or through an assistant. An import leaves such a link out and says why in its report.
