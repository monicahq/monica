title: Move somebody onto another template
type: feature
area: Contacts
published_at: 2026-11-25T14:27:30Z
---
On **Edit contact**, the new **Template** panel lets you put somebody on another template, or on another version of the one they use. Before anything changes, Monica lists the answers that will go to Unused answers, the ones that will come back, and the reminders that will stop or start again.

Nothing you wrote is deleted, and you can switch back at any time, from the same panel or from the version history. Scripts and connected assistants can preview and make the same change.
