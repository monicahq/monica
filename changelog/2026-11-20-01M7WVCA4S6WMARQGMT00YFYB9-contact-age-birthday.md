Under a contact’s name, Monica now writes how old they are. When the birthday falls within the next 7 days, it also says how old they will be and on which day. A birthday recorded without a year shows the countdown only, and one recorded without a month shows nothing.

The API gives the same two values on a contact, as `age` and `next_birthday_on`.

A birthday known only by its year no longer shows an age in the family and relationship blocks, since the age could be a year out.
