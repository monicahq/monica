The dashboard of a vault is now a single column at every screen size. Your favourite people and the people you viewed recently sit above the timeline, side by side when there is room. The years are no longer a column of their own: choose one under **Period**, next to the other filters. Each life event shows its date on the left, so the title has the line to itself.
