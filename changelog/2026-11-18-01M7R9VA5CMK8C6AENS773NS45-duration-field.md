A template can now carry a **Duration** field, for how long something takes: the usual walk, the drive to somebody’s house, a training session. You type it in minutes, and Monica shows it as “45 minutes”, the same way it shows the length of a life event.

The list of field types to pick from is also quieter: each family now shows its name only.
