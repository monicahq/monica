The timeline on the dashboard of a vault now holds every life event of the vault, not only yours. What is coming up comes first, nearest first, then one year at a time, grouped by month. A list of years beside it says how many life events each one holds.

Narrow it to one person or one kind of life event, and the counts beside the years follow, so you see at once where those life events are. The list of recent life events beside the dashboard is gone, since the timeline now shows the same ones first.
