If you keep everything in one vault, you no longer have to pick it from the list of vaults every time. In Preferences, turn off **Show the list of vaults**, and Monica opens your default vault wherever it would have shown the list, including when you sign in.

While the list is off, your default vault cannot be deleted, since it is where Monica takes you. Turn the list back on to delete it.
