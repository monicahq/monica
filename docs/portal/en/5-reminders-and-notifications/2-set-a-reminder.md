# Set a reminder

Ask Monica to tell you about something to do with a person, on a day you choose, once or again and again.

Say Joey has an audition next week, and you want to remember to ask him how it went. You add a reminder on Joey’s page, and Monica tells you on the day.

A reminder always belongs to a contact. Monica tells you on the day itself, at the time you choose, once on each channel you switched on. It never sends anything ahead of the day.

## Birthdays and other dates

You do not need this page for a birthday. When you write a date in a contact’s section, such as **Birthday**, tick **Remind me about this date**, and Monica builds the reminder from the date itself. @doc(start.first-contact) shows how. A reminder built this way follows the date: change the birthday, and the reminder moves with it.

Use the steps below for everything else.

## Add a reminder

::::steps
:::step title="Open the form"
On Joey’s page, choose the **Overview** tab. In the **Reminders** panel, choose **Add**. A short form opens in the panel.

For the full form, choose **All of Joey’s reminders** at the bottom of the panel, then **Add a reminder**.
:::

:::step title="Say what it is about"
In **What it should say**, type `Ask Joey how the audition went`. This is the text of the notification you receive.

::screenshot{label="The Add a reminder form, with What it should say, Date, Local time, How often and Time zone" src="1-contacts.reminders.new.webp"}
:::

:::step title="Choose the day"
In **Date**, choose the first day you want to be told. It cannot be in the past, and if you choose today, the time you pick must still be to come.
:::

:::step title="Choose how often"
In **How often**, choose **Once and no more**, **Every week**, **Every month** or **Every year**. For a repeating reminder, **Every so many** sets the gap: **Every month** with `3` tells you every three months.
:::

:::step title="Choose the time"
In **Local time**, choose the hour you want to hear about it. The default is 9:00. In the short form, choose **Change** next to **Notification time** to see the time and the time zone.
:::

:::step title="Add it"
Choose **Add the reminder**, or **Add reminder** in the short form.
:::
::::

Monica says **The reminder is added**, and lists it in the contact’s **Reminders** panel and in the vault’s **Reminders**.

## Time zones

Each reminder has its own time zone, set to yours when you add it. It keeps that zone afterwards, even if you change your own time zone later or travel. A reminder for 9:00 in New York arrives at 9:00 in New York, wherever you are.

Choose another zone in **Time zone** when the day belongs to somewhere else, for example a friend’s birthday on the other side of the world.

## Change or delete a reminder

On the contact’s reminders page, open the menu of the reminder and choose **Edit**. You can change anything on the form. Changing the date, the time, the time zone or how often cancels the dates still to come and plans new ones from the new schedule. Answers you already gave are kept.

A reminder built from a date field has no date of its own to edit. Change the date in the contact’s section instead, or untick **Remind me about this date** to stop it.

:::warning
Deleting a reminder cannot be undone. Nothing further is sent, and dates still waiting for an answer are dropped. The answers you already gave are kept.
:::

To delete it, open the menu of the reminder and choose **Delete**, then **Delete the reminder**.

## Try it before the day

To see what a reminder looks like when it arrives, open the vault’s **Reminders**, choose **More** on the reminder, then **Send me a test** and **Send it**. Monica emails it to you straight away. Nothing else changes, and the real one still arrives on the day.
