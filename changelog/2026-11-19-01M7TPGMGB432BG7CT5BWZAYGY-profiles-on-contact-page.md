type: improvement
area: Contacts

---
A person named in a profile, such as the owner or the vet of an animal, is now a link to their contact page. The contact page lists, under **Profiles**, every profile that names them, with what they are to it, such as **Owner**, and each one opens on its own page. Assistants and the API can read that list too.
