# People

Every person you record in Monica is a contact, with a page of their own. These pages cover what you do with contacts from day to day. Each contact lives in a vault, and @doc(concepts.model) explains how the two fit together.

1. @doc(contacts.add). Write somebody down, as a full contact or as a partial one.
2. @doc(contacts.edit). Fill in a section, add a second phone number, or correct a name.
3. @doc(contacts.list). Search, filter and sort the people in a vault, and choose the columns the list shows.
4. @doc(contacts.photo). Give a contact a photo instead of their initials.
5. @doc(profiles.add). Record a pet, and the people who look after it.

If you have never added anybody, @doc(start.first-contact) walks you through your first person from start to finish.
