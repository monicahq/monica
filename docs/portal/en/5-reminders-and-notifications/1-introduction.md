# Reminders and notifications

A reminder decides what Monica tells you and when: Phoebe’s birthday every year, or a call about the flat on Bedford Street next Tuesday. A notification channel decides how it reaches you: in the app, by email, or, where the instance offers them, on Telegram or by text message. You set up your channels once, and every reminder uses them.

1. @doc(reminders.create). Ask Monica to tell you about something, once or on a schedule.
2. @doc(reminders.answer). Say whether you remembered, and find what is coming up.
3. @doc(reminders.how-they-work). When exactly you are told, and what can delay it.
4. @doc(notifications.channels). Switch channels on or off, and connect Telegram or a phone.
5. @doc(notifications.inbox). Find what Monica told you, without leaving the app.

If you followed @doc(start.first-contact), you already have a reminder: Rachel’s birthday.
