# Troubleshooting

Find what went wrong in the words you would use, and the page that sorts it out.

- **Monica does not let me in.** @doc(troubleshoot.cannot-sign-in)
- **I lost the phone with my two factor codes.** @doc(security.recovery-codes)
- **I forgot my password.** @doc(signin.forgot-password)
- **An email from Monica never came.** @doc(troubleshoot.no-email)
- **An email says somebody signed in.** @doc(security.suspicious-activity)
- **A reminder did not reach me.** @doc(troubleshoot.reminder-not-received)
- **Monica says I cannot add any more.** @doc(troubleshoot.limit-reached)
- **I cannot find somebody I added.** @doc(contacts.list) explains search and filters. A partial contact appears only under **Partial contacts**.
- **The same person is in Monica twice.** @doc(duplicates.merge)
- **My contact still shows the old sections after I changed the template.** @doc(templates.apply-new-version)
- **A sharing link no longer opens.** It expired or was revoked. See @doc(sharing.create-link).
