Until now, deleting people or changing their category meant opening each of them, one after the other. Now the People list has a box at the start of every row. Tick the people you want, or the box in the heading to take the whole page, and a bar offers to change their category or to delete them. Deleting asks first and names who goes. After a change of category, Undo puts everybody’s categories back as they were.
