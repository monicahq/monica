Until now, changing a template meant opening each contact one by one. From the statistics of a list, you can now move every record of a template onto another one, or update every record still on an older version to the newest one. Monica first shows what will happen, such as how many values will be set aside and which reminders will pause, and nothing is deleted. Each record keeps its version history, so any of them can be put back.
