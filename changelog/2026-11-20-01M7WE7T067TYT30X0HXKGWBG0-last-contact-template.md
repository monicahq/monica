title: Deleting Basic contact no longer blocks new vaults
type: fix
area: Templates
published_at: 2026-11-20T12:17:28Z
---
Deleting **Basic contact** used to leave your vaults with no template for new contacts, and Monica then refused to create any new vault, although your other contact templates were still there. A vault now moves to the next template Monica supplied, such as **Essentials**, or to one of your own. Vaults already caught in this are repaired. Your last template for contacts can no longer be deleted, and the template screen says why.
