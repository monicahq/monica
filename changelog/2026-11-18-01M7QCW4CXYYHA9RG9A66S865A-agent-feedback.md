When an assistant connected to Monica meets a tool that fails, is missing or misleads it, it can now
send a report to the people who run your Monica, with what it tried and the fix it suggests. It
describes your request in its own words and leaves names and details from your vaults out. The call
appears in your MCP history like any other.

Administrators of an instance find these reports under Agent feedback in the admin space, where they
can accept them, mark them as fixed, dismiss them, leave notes for each other, and copy a report to
paste into an issue tracker.
