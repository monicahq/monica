# Welcome to the Monica guide

Monica helps you remember the people in your life: who they are, how they are connected, what happened to them, and when to get in touch.

## What Monica is

Monica is an open source personal relationship manager. Think of it as a private notebook about the people around you. It is not a social network: the people you write about are never told, and nobody else can open your account.

Say you want to remember that Rachel Green’s birthday is on 5 May, and that she is Ross Geller’s friend. You record both once. Monica keeps them on Rachel’s page and reminds you of her birthday every year.

## What Monica holds

- **People.** Everyone you want to remember, each with a page of their own. Monica calls them contacts.
- **Relationships.** How two people are connected. Ross is Rachel’s **friend**, and Monica is Ross’s **sibling**.
- **Life events.** Things that happened to somebody, such as a move, a new job or a wedding, in the order they happened.
- **Reminders.** Dates Monica tells you about, once or every year.
- **Templates.** The shape of each page: which sections a contact has, and which fields each section holds. You can change them to fit what you want to keep.

Everything you record lives in a vault, a compartment you can keep for one part of your life. @doc(concepts.model) explains how these pieces fit together.

## Where Monica runs

You can use Monica on monicahq.com, or on an instance: a copy of Monica that somebody runs on their own server. This guide applies to both. Where the two differ, the page says so.
