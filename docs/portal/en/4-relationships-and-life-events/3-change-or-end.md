# Change or end a relationship

Relationships change. Ross and Rachel were together from November 1995, and broke up in February 1997. Rather than delete what they were, you record when it started and when it ended, and Monica keeps it as a past relationship.

## Open a relationship

::::steps
:::step title="Open the Relationships tab"
On Ross’s page, choose the **Relationships** tab.
:::

:::step title="Show the controls"
Next to the group the relationship is in, such as **Romantic**, choose **Manage**. Each row now shows **Edit** and **Delete**.
:::

:::step title="Edit the row"
On Rachel’s row, choose **Edit**. The **Edit this relationship** screen opens.
:::
::::

## Record when it started and ended

::::steps
:::step title="Enter the start"
In **Started on**, enter 9 November 1995.
:::

:::step title="Mark it as ended"
Tick **This relationship has ended**, then enter 20 February 1997 in **Ended on**.

::screenshot{label="The Edit this relationship screen between Ross Geller and Rachel Green, as partners, started on 9 November 1995, ended on 20 February 1997, with This relationship has ended ticked" src="1-contacts.relationships.edit.webp"}
:::

:::step title="Save"
Choose **Save changes**.
:::
::::

Monica says **The relationship is changed**. Rachel’s row now shows **Ended** and the two dates, on both pages.

Both dates are optional, and the end cannot fall before the start. **Ended on** counts only when **This relationship has ended** is ticked: untick it, and Monica clears the end date and treats the relationship as current again.

**Immediate family** and the **Family tree** show current partners only, so an ended partnership, marriage or engagement no longer appears in them. A parent stays a parent: ending a parent and child relationship does not take it off the family tree.

## Change the type or the direction

In **Relationship**, choose another type, or the same type read the other way round. Each choice is written as a sentence, such as **Ross Geller is Rachel Green’s partner**, so you can see who holds which role before you save. A relationship that happened again later, like Ross and Rachel getting back together in 2004, is best recorded as a second relationship, so each period keeps its own dates.

## Name somebody you did not know

When the other person was written down without a name, or without a record of their own, the edit screen also lets you fix that:

- Choose **I know their name now** and type their name.
- Tick **Create a contact record for this person** to move them from the partial contacts into your list of people.

## Remove a relationship

On the row, choose **Delete**. Monica asks **Remove this relationship?** and explains that the link is taken off both records, and that neither person is deleted. Choose **Remove it** to confirm.

The two people stay exactly as they were, with everything else written about them. Only the link between them goes. To keep a record of a relationship that is over, mark it as ended instead.
