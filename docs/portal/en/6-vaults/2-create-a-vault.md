# Create a vault

Start a new vault for another part of your life, such as the people you know through work.

Say you want Monica to keep your colleagues apart from your friends and family. You create a vault called **Work**, and add your colleagues there instead of in **Personal**. @doc(vaults.introduction) explains when a second vault helps.

## Before you start

On monicahq.com, the free offer holds one vault, and your **Personal** vault already uses it. The form then says **You are at the ceiling of the free offer**, and **Create vault** stays unavailable until you have full access. On an instance somebody runs on their own server, there is no limit on vaults.

## Create the vault

::::steps
:::step title="Open the form"
Go to **Your vaults**, the screen you land on after signing in, and choose **New vault**. **Start another vault**, next to your vaults, opens the same form.
:::

:::step title="Name it"
In **Name**, type `Work`. Keep it short, since it appears in the vault switcher and on the list of vaults. In **Description**, you can say in a sentence or two what the vault holds.

::screenshot{label="The Create a vault form, with Name, Description, the colour choices and Make this the default vault" src="1-vaults.new.webp"}
:::

:::step title="Pick a colour"
Under **Colour**, pick one. It only helps you tell vaults apart at a glance.
:::

:::step title="Choose whether it becomes the default"
Tick **Make this the default vault** if new records should go to this vault from now on. Otherwise, your current default stays the default.
:::

:::step title="Create it"
Choose **Create vault**.
:::
::::

Monica says **The vault is created** and shows it on **Your vaults**. Open it to start adding people.

## What the new vault starts with

A new vault starts empty. For new contacts, it uses **Basic contact**, the template Monica prepared for you, and for animals, **Basic animal**. When your account has more than one contact template, the form also shows **What a new contact starts from**, with a **Default template** list, so you can choose another from the start. You can change it later in the vault’s settings.

## The default vault

One vault in your account is the default. Anything created without a vault being chosen goes there. Your first vault is the default until you tick **Make this the default vault** when you create another one. The vault that is the default is marked **Default** on **Your vaults**.

The vault settings have no way to make an existing vault the default. You choose it when you create a vault.
