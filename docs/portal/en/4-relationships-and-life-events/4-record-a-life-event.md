# Record something that happened

A life event is something that happened on a date, often with other people: a wedding, a move, a new job, a trip. You record it once, and it appears on the timeline of everybody who took part.

Say Monica and Chandler married on 17 May 2001, in a ballroom in Manhattan, with all their friends there. You add the wedding from Chandler’s page, list the guests, and it shows on each of their pages. @doc(life-events.timeline) explains how that works.

## Add a life event

Adding a life event takes two steps: choosing what happened, then filling in the details.

::::steps
:::step title="Open the panel"
On Chandler’s page, on the **Overview** tab, choose **Add a life event** above the **Timeline**.
:::

:::step title="Choose what happened"
The panel says **Step 1 of 2 · Choose what happened**. Pick a category, then **Marriage**. To find a type faster, type in **Search every type**.
:::

:::step title="Enter the date"
The panel moves to **Step 2 of 2 · Fill in the details**. In **When did it happen**, enter 17 May 2001.
:::

:::step title="Say who was there"
Under **Who was there**, Chandler is already listed, because the event is on his page. Choose **Add someone**, search for Monica Geller, and pick her. Add the others the same way.
:::

:::step title="Add the details you want to keep"
Under **Details**, fill in **Summary**, the line the timeline shows, and any other piece you want: **Description**, **Place**, **Duration**, and the fields of the type, such as **Kind of ceremony** for a marriage. Choose a piece to add it, and **Remove** to take it away. Everything here is optional.

::screenshot{label="The life event panel for Monica and Chandler’s wedding, with the Marriage type, the date, the guests under Who was there, and the Summary, Description and Place details" src="1-contacts.lifeEvents.edit.webp"}
:::

:::step title="Add it to the timeline"
Choose **Add to the timeline**.
:::
::::

Monica confirms that the marriage is on the timeline of everybody you listed, and marks the new entry **Just added**.

## Change a life event

On the timeline, choose **Edit** on the entry. The panel opens with everything you wrote, and **Save changes** writes your changes to every timeline the event is on. The entry is then marked **Edited**.

To pick another type, choose **Change type**. The date and the people stay as they are. The fields that belong to the old type may not:

:::warning
If the new type does not ask for a field you filled in, what you wrote there is dropped when you save.
:::

## Delete a life event

On the timeline, choose **Delete** on the entry. Monica asks **Delete this life event?** and says whose timelines it is taken from. Choose **Delete life event** to confirm.

The event disappears from the timeline of everybody in it, not only from the page you are on. The people themselves are not affected.

Right after the deletion, the timeline shows a notice with **Undo**. Choose it to bring the event back, with its people and its details. The notice lasts until you leave or reload the page, so undo straight away if you deleted the wrong one.

## When there is no type to choose

Monica prepares a list of life event types for you when you sign up, grouped in categories. You can add your own types, add fields to them, or switch some off, from **Manage life event types** at the bottom of the first step. If the panel says **There is no life event type to choose from yet**, choose **Add one in the settings**.
