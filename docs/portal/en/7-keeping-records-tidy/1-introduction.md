# Keeping records tidy

Sooner or later, one person ends up in Monica twice. You add Phoebe by hand, then your phone syncs its own Phoebe Buffay. Or you write down "Pheebs" from a relationship form and forget she is already there. Monica spots people who look alike, and lets you make two records one without losing anything either of them held.

1. @doc(duplicates.review). See who Monica thinks might be the same person, and tell it when they are not.
2. @doc(duplicates.merge). Make two records one, choose what to keep where they disagree, and undo it if you need to.

Both work inside one vault. Monica never compares people across vaults, and never merges anybody on its own.
