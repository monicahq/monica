# Merge two contacts

When one person has two records, merge them. One record stays, and the other is absorbed into it: its answers, its photo, its reminders and its sharing links come over. Nothing is thrown away, and you can undo the merge.

Say your phone synced a second Phoebe Buffay, with her work email, next to the Phoebe you wrote by hand. You keep your own record, and bring the other one into it.

## Choose which record stays

The record whose page you start from is the one that stays. Its name, its address and its template are kept. So start from the better of the two, usually the one you filled in yourself.

The contact that stands for you can stay, but it can never be absorbed into another contact.

## Move relationships and life events first

A merge brings over what is written on the other record, but not its relationships or the life events it takes part in. Those stay with the absorbed record, out of sight.

Before you merge, open the **Relationships** tab of the record that goes, and add each of its relationships again on the record that stays, then remove them from the one that goes. For its life events, open each one from the record that goes, and add the person who stays under **Who was there**. @doc(relationships.edit) and @doc(life-events.record) show how.

## Merge them

::::steps
:::step title="Start from the record that stays"
On Phoebe’s page, open the menu next to **Edit contact** and choose **Merge**. If Monica suggested the pair, you can instead choose **Compare them** on the list of possible duplicates, as @doc(duplicates.review) explains.
:::

:::step title="Pick the other record"
The screen **Merge somebody into Phoebe Buffay** lists everybody else in the vault, under **Who is the same person?** Choose **Compare them** next to the second record.

::screenshot{label="The Merge somebody into Phoebe Buffay screen, listing the other people of the vault under Who is the same person?, each with Compare them" src="1-contacts.merge.new.webp"}
:::

:::step title="Settle what they disagree on"
Monica shows the two records side by side. Where both hold a different answer to the same question, it says **These two records disagree here. Which one is right?** Pick the answer to keep.
:::

:::step title="Settle what they both hold"
Where both hold the same entry, such as the same email address, Monica shows **The same entry twice**. Choose **Keep it once** or **Keep both entries**.
:::

:::step title="Check what comes over"
Under **What comes over**, Monica counts the answers, entries, reminders and sharing links that move to the record that stays, and the other records that will point to it instead.
:::

:::step title="Merge"
Read **This absorbs a contact**, then choose **Merge Phoebe Buffay into Phoebe Buffay**, with the names of your two records. To go back, choose **Choose somebody else**.
:::
::::

Monica says **Phoebe Buffay is one contact now**, and that everything the other record held came over. When the two records disagree nowhere, the comparison says **Nothing to decide**, and you only need to confirm.

## What happens to the other record

- It no longer appears in the vault.
- Its answers, its photo, its reminders and its sharing links now belong to the record that stays, and its sharing links keep working. A field elsewhere that named it, such as a pet’s **Caregivers**, now names the record that stays.
- Answers to questions the record that stays does not ask are set aside, not thrown away. Monica lists them under **Set aside rather than shown** before you merge, and you find them later in **Answers to questions no longer asked**. @doc(contacts.edit) shows where.
- Its old address still works. It opens a page saying where the person is now, with a button to open the record that stays.
- If the record that stays was a partial contact, it becomes a full one.

## Undo a merge

After a merge, the record that stays shows **This record absorbed another**, with the name of the absorbed contact.

::::steps
:::step title="Open the record that stays"
Open the contact the other record was merged into.
:::

:::step title="Undo it"
Choose **Undo the merge**.
:::
::::

Monica says **The merge is undone**: both people are back, each holding what they held before. Answers, reminders and sharing links go back to the record they came from.

The undo stays available with no time limit, but only for the latest merge into a record, and only while both records still exist. Once the record that stays is deleted, the merge can no longer be undone.
