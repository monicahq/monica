# Read everything that happened to a possession

The story of one of your @doc(customise.possessions, "possessions") is spread over its sections: when it was bought, insured, valued, repaired, lent and moved. The **Timeline** puts that story back together in one list, the newest first.

Michael Scott bought a Holiday Armadillo costume from Party City in June 2025, kept it in the hall closet, had the zip replaced in December, insured it in March, lent it to Dwight for the office party in October and moved it to the storage space when it came back. Each of those was recorded in its own section. The timeline shows them all, in order.

Open a possession and choose **Timeline** in the list of its sections. The number beside it says how many entries it holds.

## What the timeline shows

Each entry comes from one record of another section:

- a **transaction**, on the day it happened, with its total;
- an **insurance** record, on the day its cover starts, with what it insures the possession for;
- a **valuation**, on its date, with its amount and how much it moved since the one before;
- a **provenance** event, on its date, as precisely as it was recorded, such as **About 2024**;
- a **maintenance** record, on the day it was done, with what it cost;
- a **loan**, once when it started and once more when it came back;
- a **location**, on the day the possession arrived there.

A few entries carry a mark: **Acquisition** for the transaction that brought the possession in, **Current value** for the latest valuation, and **Here now** for the place it is kept in today.

Entries are grouped by the year they happened in. Those without a date come last, under **Date unknown**.

## Show only one section

Above the entries, a chip names each section that holds something, with how many entries it gives. Choose one to read only that section, or **All** to read everything again. The choice stays when you reload the page or share its link.

## Correct an entry

Nothing is stored on the timeline itself: each entry is read from its section every time you open it. To correct an entry, choose its title. Its section opens, where you change or delete the record, and the timeline follows.

A loan you record in the possession's provenance, or a purchase linked to a provenance event, shows once on the timeline, not twice.
