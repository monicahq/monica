# Say what state your things are in

A possession condition says what state a thing is in: **New**, **Used**, **Broken**. A @doc(customise.possession-types, "type") says what a thing is, a @doc(customise.locations, "location") says where it is, a @doc(customise.possession-tags, "tag") says what you want to remember about it, and a condition says how it has held up. Conditions belong to your account, so every vault uses the same list.

Open your settings, and under **Possessions** choose **Possession conditions**.

Your account starts with six, from best to worst: **New**, **Like new**, **Used**, **Worn**, **Damaged** and **Broken**. They are written in the language you used when you created your account, and you can change, move or delete any of them.

Giving a possession its condition comes in a later release. For now you prepare the list.

## Add a condition

::::steps
:::step title="Name it"
Under **Add a condition**, type `Mint` in **Name**. A condition holds up to 50 characters.
:::

:::step title="Pick its icon and colour"
Choose **Icon** to pick a glyph, or **No icon** to go without one. Choose **Colour** to pick one of the eight colours. The preview below shows the condition as it will look.
:::

:::step title="Add it"
Choose **Add condition**, or press Enter. The condition joins the end of the list under **Your conditions**.
:::
::::

Two conditions cannot share a name, even written in different capitals: with **Used** in the list, `used` is refused.

## Put them in order

The list reads from best to worst, and the order is yours. Drag a condition by its handle to another place, or use its **Move up** and **Move down** buttons, which also work from the keyboard. After a move, the focus stays on the condition you moved.

## Change or delete a condition

Choose **Edit** on a condition to rename it, or to change its icon or its colour, then **Save changes**. **Cancel**, or the Escape key, leaves it as it was. The condition keeps its place in the list.

Choose **Delete** on a condition, and confirm. The deletion cannot be undone, and the conditions after it move up to close the gap. If you delete all six, they are not added back.

## From the API or an assistant

Everything on this screen can also be done through the [API reference](/docs/api) and by an assistant connected as the [MCP guide](/docs/mcp) explains.
