title: Open and correct a profile
type: feature
area: Vaults
published_at: 2026-11-19T18:59:14Z
---
A profile, such as one of your animals, now opens on a page of its own from its list. The pages of its
template sit in a column on the left, and each section shows its fields line by line, with **Not recorded**
where nothing is written yet. **Edit** opens the same form you added it with, filled in, so you can correct
anything. Assistants and the API can read and correct a profile too.
