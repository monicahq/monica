A partial contact is somebody you know through another person, kept off the list of people and out of the count of the free plan. Their page now says so in a band at the top, with a button to make them a full contact. Before, the only way was to open the relationship again, or to write the person down a second time and merge the two.

It works the other way too. On the edit page of a full contact, **Make a partial contact** takes them off the list of people without changing anything written about them. Monica asks first, and says that they also leave the address books and the birthday calendar your phones synchronise.
