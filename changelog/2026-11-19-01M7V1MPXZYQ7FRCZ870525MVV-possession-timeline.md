A possession now has a **Timeline**: everything recorded in its other sections, in one list, the newest first and grouped by year. Purchases, insurance, valuations, provenance, repairs, loans and moves each give an entry, with its date and, where there is one, its amount. Choose a section to read only that, and choose an entry to correct it where it was recorded. Assistants and the API can read the timeline too.
