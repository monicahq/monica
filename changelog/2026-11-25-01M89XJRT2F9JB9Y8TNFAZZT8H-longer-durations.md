title: Durations now go up to a year and read in weeks, days and hours
type: improvement
area: Contacts
published_at: 2026-11-25T17:55:44Z
---
Until now, a duration stopped at 100,000 minutes, about 69 days, and was always shown as a number of minutes. Now a duration can last up to a year, and it reads the way you would say it: 90 minutes is shown as 1 hour 30 minutes, and three weeks as 3 weeks. Something that comes back, such as a visit every three months, is still best recorded as a reminder.
