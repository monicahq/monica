# Your account and security

Your account belongs to you alone. These pages cover how you sign in, how you keep somebody else out, and how you manage the account itself. You manage all of it in your settings, under **You**.

Some protection is on from the start, without you doing anything. Your password is stored scrambled, so nobody can read it back. Repeated wrong passwords are slowed down. Sign in links work once, for a few minutes. And Monica emails you when somebody fails to sign in, signs in without a password, or signs in from a new place, so you would notice what you did not do.
