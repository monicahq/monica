title: An empty list of your own records shows what it will hold
type: improvement
area: Vaults
published_at: 2026-11-19T17:47:58Z
---
When a vault holds nothing yet of a kind of record you created, such as Animals, the list now shows the
shape it will take, marked with the icon and the colour you chose for that kind. Under it, the
description you wrote for the kind says what goes there, and a button adds the first one.
