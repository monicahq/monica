# Find what you wrote

As @doc(knowledge.introduction, "Knowledge") grows, two things help you find your way back: search, and the contents.

## Search

Type a few words in **Search notes and lists**, at the top of the folder column, then press Enter. The column keeps only the notes and lists holding every word you typed, in their title, their text, their description or any cell, with their folders unfolded.

Search forgives small differences: capital letters and accents do not matter, the start of a word is enough, and a typing mistake in a longer word is tolerated. Phoebe Buffay finds her song list by typing **smelly** or even **smely**.

When nothing matches, the column says so and repeats what you typed. Clear the field and press Enter to see everything again.

## Contents

**Contents**, at the top of the folder column, lists every folder with its notes and lists, how many items each list holds and when each was last edited. Each note also shows its headings: click one to open the note straight at that heading.

Search covers Knowledge only. The search at the top of the vault finds people and life events, not notes or lists.
