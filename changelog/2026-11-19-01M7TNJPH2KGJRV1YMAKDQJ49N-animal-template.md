Every account now has an **Animal** template beside **Basic animal**. It keeps more about an animal on two pages: its character, what it likes and fears, how to approach it, photos, its food and routine, instructions for whoever looks after it, its health, and its identification and insurance documents. Every field is optional, so it works as well for a friend’s cat as for your own dog.

When you add an animal, pick the template it starts from. Basic animal stays the default, and nothing changes for the animals you already wrote down. An animal written on Animal shows its species, breed and date of birth under its name.
