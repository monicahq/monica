Monica used to list people by family name in every language. The list now follows the language you read Monica in.

In Icelandic, Sinhala, Tamil and Vietnamese, people are listed by given name. In Dutch, German, French, Spanish and Portuguese, a family name starting with a word such as van, von, de or dos is listed under the word after it. In Turkish, Irmak and İpek are listed under their own letters. In Japanese, somebody whose name has a pronunciation written in hiragana or katakana is listed by that reading.

If you read Monica in one of these languages, your list changes order once, after this update. No name has changed, and a search finds the same people as before.
