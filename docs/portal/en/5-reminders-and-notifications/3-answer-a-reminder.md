# Answer a reminder

When a reminder’s day comes, tell Monica whether you did what it was about. Your answer closes that date, stops anything still waiting to be sent for it, and is kept so you can see over time whom you keep in touch with.

## Where reminders wait

Each date a reminder falls on waits for your answer in three places:

- **On the contact’s page.** The **Reminders** panel of the **Overview** tab lists their dates, under **All**, **Overdue**, **Today** and **Upcoming**.
- **On the contact’s reminders page.** Choose **All of Chandler’s reminders** at the bottom of that panel. It adds a **History** of the dates already answered, and shows how many of them you remembered and forgot over the last 12 months.
- **In the vault.** In the vault’s sidebar, choose **Reminders**. **Coming up** lists every date still to come in the vault, with overdue ones first. Under **Grouped by**, choose **When it is sent** or **Contact**.

::screenshot{label="The vault’s Reminders screen on the Coming up tab, with one overdue reminder about Chandler Bing showing I remembered and I forgot, then the upcoming reminders" src="1-reminders.index.webp"}

The vault’s **Reminders** also has two other tabs. **Sent** lists the reminders already delivered, and on which channel. **History** lists the answers you gave.

## Answer a date

On the date, choose one of the two answers:

- **I remembered**, if you did what the reminder was about. You can choose it from the day itself.
- **I forgot**, if the day went by. You can choose it from the day after.

Monica says **Kept as remembered** or **Kept as forgotten**, and moves the date to the history.

An answer is final. Once you answer a date, you cannot change the answer, and nothing still waiting to be sent for that date goes out. The reminder itself carries on: a yearly birthday comes back next year, with a new date to answer.

A date you leave unanswered stays under **Overdue**. It does not stop the next dates from coming.

## When a reminder stops

A reminder can stop on its own, and its page then says why:

- **Nothing is sent while the record is deleted.** The contact it is about was deleted.
- **This reminder has no date to follow.** It read a date from a field, and the date was cleared. Write the date again on the contact, with **Remind me about this date** ticked, to build a new reminder.
- **This reminder has no field to read.** The field it read was taken off the contact’s template. Put the field back, and the reminder starts again on its own.
