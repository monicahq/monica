area: Possessions

Lending something on its loans screen never marked it as lent in the list of possessions, and a possession could be marked as lent with no loan behind it. A possession now shows as lent while it has an active loan to somebody, and Lent is no longer a status you choose. Possessions that were marked as lent are now owned.
