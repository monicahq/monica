Until now, your security settings listed the phones signed in through the Monica app in one place and the AI assistants you connected in another, and an assistant you had allowed once could come back without asking. Now every app you allow through your sign-in page appears in one list, Connected apps, with what it may do and when it was last used, and you disconnect any of them the same way. Monica also asks you every time an app connects, even one you allowed before.
