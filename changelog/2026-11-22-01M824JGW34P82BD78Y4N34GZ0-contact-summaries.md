Ask your assistant who is in a vault, and it now gets every person in one go, with their name and whether they are a partial contact. It can keep only partial contacts or only full ones, and add archived contacts when you ask. Checking the list first also helps it avoid adding somebody who already exists. The same list is available through the API.
