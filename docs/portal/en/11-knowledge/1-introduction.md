# Knowledge

Knowledge is where you keep everything you want to remember that is not a person or a possession: the paint colours of the kitchen, the books you have read, the plan for a trip, the restaurants worth going back to. It sits in the sidebar of each vault, under **Knowledge**.

Monica Geller keeps the recipe tweaks she swears by, the wine her guests liked and the dates of every dinner party she has thrown. None of that belongs on a contact. All of it belongs in Knowledge.

## Three things to work with

- A **folder** groups what belongs together, such as Home, Travel or Kitchen.
- A **note** is free text: a few lines or a long page, with headings, bullets and tasks.
- A **list** is a table you design yourself, such as restaurants with a city, a cuisine and a note.

Every note and every list sits in one folder. Knowledge belongs to @doc(vaults.introduction, "a vault"), like everything else, so the Knowledge of your **Personal** vault never shows in your **Work** vault.

## Getting started

The first time you open **Knowledge**, the page explains these three things and offers **Write a first note**, **Start a list** and **Create a folder**. You do not need to make a folder first: the first note or list you start gets a folder called **General** to hold it, and you can rename it later.

1. @doc(knowledge.folders). Group your notes and lists, rename and reorder folders, and delete one safely.
2. @doc(knowledge.notes). Write and format a note, add links, and tick tasks.
3. @doc(knowledge.lists). Design the columns of a list and fill it.
4. @doc(knowledge.find). Search everything and browse the contents.

## What Knowledge does not do yet

A note or a list cannot link to a contact, a possession or another note yet. Write the name as plain text for now. Knowledge is also not included when you share a contact or a vault.

## From an assistant or a script

Everything you do on these screens, an assistant connected to your account can do too, and so can a script using the API. Assistants read and change the same folders, notes and lists, so what you write here is also what they know. See @doc(security.assistants) and @doc(security.api-keys).
