Until now, only the Monica app for Android could sign you in from your phone. Now the Monica app for iPhone signs you in the same way, through your usual sign-in page opened in your phone’s browser, on app.monicahq.com or on any Monica someone runs on their own server, with nothing for its administrator to set up. Your iPhone then appears under Mobile apps in your security settings, next to your other phones, where you can sign it out if you lose it.
