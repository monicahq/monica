# Keep and renew your recovery codes

Recovery codes get you in when you have lost the phone that gives you two factor codes. When you turn on @doc(security.two-factor, "two factor authentication"), Monica gives you eight codes of ten characters. Each one signs you in once.

## Save them

Monica shows the codes once, on **Your recovery codes**, right after you turn two factor authentication on or ask for new ones.

::::steps
:::step title="Copy them"
Choose **Copy to the clipboard**, or write them down.
:::

:::step title="Put them somewhere safe"
Keep them away from your phone: in a password manager, or printed and put away. If they are on the phone you lose, they cannot help you.
:::

:::step title="Confirm"
Choose **I have saved them**.
:::
::::

:::warning
Leave that screen and the codes are gone. Monica never shows them again: afterwards, **Security and access** only says how many are left.
:::

## Use one

On the **One more step** screen, type a recovery code in **Six digit code** instead of the code from your app, and choose **Verify**. That code then stops working. Once in, set up your authenticator app on your new phone.

## Get new codes

Ask for new codes when you have used several, or lost the list. On **Security and access**, under **Recovery codes**, choose **Get new codes**, then confirm.

:::warning
The codes you had stop working straight away. Save the new ones before you leave the screen.
:::

When you have used every code, **Security and access** says **You have used every code. Ask for new ones before you need them.**
