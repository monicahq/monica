# Tag the things you own

A possession tag is a word you will put on the things you own, to sort them across their types and where they are kept: **Insured**, **To sell**, **Fragile**. A @doc(customise.possession-types, "type") says what a thing is and a @doc(customise.locations, "location") says where it is; a tag says what you want to remember about it. Tags belong to your account, so every vault uses the same list.

Open your settings, and under **Possessions** choose **Possession tags**.

You put tags on a possession when you @doc(customise.possessions, "add it"), and a tag you type there that the list does not hold yet joins it.

## Add a tag

::::steps
:::step title="Name it"
Under **Add a tag**, type `Insured` in **Name**. A tag holds up to 50 characters.

::screenshot{label="The Possession tags screen, with the Add a tag form above the list of six existing tags" src="1-settings.possessionTags.index.webp"}
:::

:::step title="Pick its colour"
Choose one of the eight colours under **Colour**. The preview below shows the tag as it will look.
:::

:::step title="Add it"
Choose **Add tag**, or press Enter. The tag joins the list under **Your tags**, sorted by name.
:::
::::

Two tags cannot share a name, even written in different capitals: with **Insured** in the list, `insured` is refused.

## Change or delete a tag

Choose **Edit** on a tag to rename it or change its colour, then **Save changes**. **Cancel**, or the Escape key, leaves it as it was. Changing only the capitals of its own name is allowed.

Choose **Delete** on a tag, and confirm. The confirmation says how many possessions carry the tag; they lose it and keep everything else. The deletion cannot be undone.
