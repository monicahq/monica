# Choose your language, formats and time zone

Open your settings and choose **Preferences**. Each option saves as soon as you pick it, and follows you to every device you sign in from.

::screenshot{label="The Preferences screen, with Language, Time format, Date format, Number format, Links and the time zone picker" src="1-settings.preferences.index.webp"}

## Language and formats

- **Language** is the language Monica is shown in. Monica speaks 31 languages. The names of your contacts are also sorted the way that language sorts them.
- **Time format** is **24-hour**, as in 14:00, or **12-hour**, as in 2:00 PM.
- **Date format** decides the order of the day, the month and the year. The list shows each choice as an example date, such as 21/09/2026. The first one follows the usual order of your language.
- **Number format** decides the separators in a number. The list shows each choice as an example, such as 1,234.56. The first one follows your language.
- **Links** decides what the address of a vault or a person is written with: **Readable names**, such as `/vaults/personal/contacts/rachel-green`, or **Identifiers**, a string of letters and digits that never changes with a name. Addresses you already saved keep working either way.

The line at the foot of the section shows the time and the date as Monica will write them.

## Time zone

Search for the city you are in, or its country, and pick it from the list. You can also click the map, or choose **Use my device** to take the time zone your browser reports.

Your time zone decides how dates and times are written for you. It does not move the reminders you already set: each keeps the time zone it was created with, as @doc(reminders.how-they-work) explains.

## The language on the sign in screen

The language you pick on the sign in screen applies to that screen and to the rest of that visit, even over the one saved here. To change it for good, change it here.
