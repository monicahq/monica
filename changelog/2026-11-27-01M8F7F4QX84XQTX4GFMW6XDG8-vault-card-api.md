Until now, an app connected to Monica could list your vaults but not say when you last opened one, who is in it, or how many dates are coming up, and it could not show the pictures of your contacts. Now the API gives each vault the time it was last opened, up to four of the people in it with their pictures, and how many reminders are due before the month is out, the same as the cards on your vaults page. It can also list your vaults in the order you last opened them.
