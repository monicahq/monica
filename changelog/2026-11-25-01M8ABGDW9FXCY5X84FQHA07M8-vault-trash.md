title: Deleted things now wait 30 days in the trash of their vault
type: feature
area: Vault
published_at: 2026-11-25T21:59:06Z
---
Until now, deleting a person, a group or a life event by mistake left you with no way back, unless you noticed the undo straight away. Some things, such as an animal, could not be deleted at all.

Now whatever you delete from a vault waits 30 days in its trash, under **Vault settings**, then **Trash**. Restore it and it goes back where it was, with everything deleted at the same time: a person comes back with their relationships, life events and reminders, and a group with its members, notes and files. You can also delete something for good, or empty the trash, before the 30 days are over.
