# Change, set or remove your password

Open your settings and choose **Security and access**. The **Password** section shows what applies to your account: a form to change it, a form to set one, or a way to remove it. Each change asks you to confirm it is you first, with your password, a passkey or a link Monica emails you, and after that Monica leaves you alone for three hours by default.

Choose one you use nowhere else. A long password is better than a complicated one.

## Change your password

::::steps
:::step title="Enter the current one"
Type it in **Current password**.

::screenshot{label="The Password section of the security settings, with fields for the current, new and confirmed password and a Save button" src="1-settings.security.index.webp"}
:::

:::step title="Enter the new one"
Type it in **New password** and again in **Confirm new password**. It needs at least 8 characters.
:::

:::step title="Save"
Choose **Save**.
:::
::::

Monica says **Your password is changed.** You stay signed in, and the new password is what you type next time. Monica emails you with the subject **Your Monica password was changed**. **Last changed** under the form says when you last changed it.

Changing your password does not sign you out on your other devices.

If you forgot the current password, follow @doc(signin.forgot-password) instead.

## Set a password

An account created with an outside service or a passkey has no password. The section then says **This account has no password.** To add one, type it in **New password** and **Confirm new password**, at least 8 characters, and choose **Set a password**.

Monica says **Your password is set.** and emails you with the subject **A password was set on your Monica account**.

## Remove your password

A password is the one thing somebody can phish from you. If you sign in with a passkey, or with an outside service, you can do without it.

::::steps
:::step title="Start"
Choose **Remove the password**.
:::

:::step title="Confirm"
Read the warning, then choose **Remove the password** again. To stop, choose **Keep my password**.
:::
::::

Monica says **Your password is removed.** and emails you with the subject **Your Monica password was removed**. Your passkeys, a connected service, and a link sent to your email address still sign you in. You can set a password again at any time.

The option appears only when your account has another way in. Monica refuses otherwise, for example: **Add a passkey first. Removing your password without one would leave nothing but a link sent by email.**
