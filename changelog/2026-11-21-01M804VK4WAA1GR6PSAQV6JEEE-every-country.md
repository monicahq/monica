title: An address can be in any country
type: feature
area: Contacts
published_at: 2026-11-21T22:50:28Z
---
The country of an address now offers every country in the world, named in your language. Your favourite countries come first, and typing narrows the list, so you no longer leave a contact to add a country before you can record an address there. A place that is not on the list can still be recorded by its name.

In the Countries list of the settings, you add a favourite by picking it from the same list rather than typing its code, or by typing a name for a place that is not a country.
