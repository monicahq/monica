# Move a contact to a new template version

When a template has a newer version, the contacts already on it stay where they are until you move them. Move a contact when you want their page to show the new sections and fields, and leave them when you do not. @doc(templates.versions) explains why.

## Apply the new version

When a newer version of a contact’s template is published, their page says **Version 2 of the Friends template is ready**, with the number and the name of your template, and the note written when it was published.

::::steps
:::step title="Read the banner"
Open the contact’s page. The banner explains that nothing changes until you apply the new version, and that everything already written down stays.
:::

:::step title="Apply it"
Choose **Apply version 2**.
:::
::::

Monica says **Rachel Green is on version 2 now**, and that nothing written down was touched. The page now shows the sections and fields of version 2.

To leave a contact on their version, do nothing. The banner stays until you apply the new version.

Each contact moves on their own. There is no way to move every contact of a template at once from the web.

## What happens to what you wrote

- **Before the move**, Monica takes a snapshot of the contact, so you can go back.
- **Answers to fields version 2 still has** stay exactly where they are.
- **Answers to fields version 2 no longer has** are set aside, not deleted. They wait under **Answers to questions no longer asked**, and come back on their own if a later version has the field again. Reminders built from such a date stop, and start again when the field comes back.

## Go back to an earlier version

::::steps
:::step title="Open the contact’s version history"
On the contact’s page, open the menu next to **Edit contact** and choose **Version history**. It lists each version the contact has used, marks the one in use **Current**, and says how long each was in use.
:::

:::step title="Revert"
On the earlier version, choose **Revert**. Monica asks **Revert to version 1?**, and explains that answers in fields that version does not have are hidden, not deleted.
:::

:::step title="Confirm"
Choose **Revert** to go back, or **Stay on version 2** to keep the current one.
:::
::::

You can revert only to a published version of the same template. The move is recorded in the version history, like any other.

Under the history, a line says how many values written under other versions are hidden, with a link to **Unused answers**.
