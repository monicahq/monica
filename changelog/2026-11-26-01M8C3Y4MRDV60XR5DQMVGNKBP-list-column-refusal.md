Until now, asking an assistant or the API to show an email, phone, birthday or sensitive field as a column of the people list was refused with a message saying that no template carried the field, which was wrong. Now the message tells you that the email, phone number or birthday column shows it, or that a sensitive field cannot be shown in the list.
