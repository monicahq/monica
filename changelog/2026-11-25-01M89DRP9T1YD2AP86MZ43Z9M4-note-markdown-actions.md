Until now, getting a note out of Monica meant selecting its text and copying it by hand. Now a note in Knowledge has a **Markdown** menu next to Edit. **Copy** puts the whole note, title included, on your clipboard, ready to paste into an assistant or another editor. **View as MD** shows the text exactly as it is written, and **Download as MD** saves it as a file.
