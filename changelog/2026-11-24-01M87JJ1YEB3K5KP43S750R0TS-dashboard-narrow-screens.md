Until now, the dashboard of a vault was split into columns, and the years had a column of their own, which left little room on smaller screens. Now the dashboard is a single column at every size. Your favourite and recently viewed people sit above the timeline, and you pick a year under **Period**, next to the other filters. Each life event shows its date on the left, so its title has the whole line.
