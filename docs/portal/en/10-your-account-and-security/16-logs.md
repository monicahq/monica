# Read your activity and the emails sent to you

Open your settings and choose **Logs**. It holds two records: what was done in your account, and every email Monica sent you.

## Activity

**Activity** lists every action that touched your account, newest first. Each entry says who did it, what was done, such as **Changed their password**, when, and the internet address it came from. An entry with **No address, so this was the application itself** was done by Monica on its own, such as a nightly task.

Choose **Load more** to go further back. Entries are never changed or removed while your account exists.

Use it to check what happened when an email warned you about a sign in, as @doc(security.suspicious-activity) explains.

## Emails sent

**Emails sent** lists the latest emails Monica sent you. Choose **Browse all emails** for the full list.

Each email shows its subject, the address it went to, when it was sent, and what became of it:

- **Delivered**: your mail provider accepted it.
- **Bounced**: it could not be delivered, or sending it failed.
- **Marked as spam**: it was delivered, and marked as spam.
- **On its way**: Monica sent it, and has had no news since.

Whether an email moves past **On its way** depends on the mail service your Monica uses. Some never report back, and then every email stays **On its way**, delivered or not.

Open an email to read what was sent. Monica removes the links from the copy, since they have probably expired.

If you expected an email and it is not in the list, it was never sent. If it is in the list but not in your inbox, look in your spam folder.
