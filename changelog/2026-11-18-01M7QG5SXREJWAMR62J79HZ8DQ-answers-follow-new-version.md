title: Answers stay in place when a contact moves to a new template version
type: fix
area: Templates
published_at: 2026-11-18T14:15:07Z
---
Changing one field of a section, even only its label, set aside every answer in that section once a contact moved to the new version, and the reminders on its dates stopped. The answers to the fields you did not change now stay on the contact page, and their reminders keep running.
