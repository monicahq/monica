An address is now laid out as its country writes it on an envelope, one line after another: the postcode before the town in France, the town, state and ZIP code together in the United States. A phone number typed with its international prefix, such as +33612345678, is grouped the way its country groups it. Other numbers read as you typed them, and nothing you saved changes.

A country you add to your Countries list can carry its two letter code, so its addresses take its layout too.
