Until now, nothing told you how many people or animals in a vault followed which template, or which questions anybody actually answered. Each list now has a **Statistics** button. It counts the records of the vault by template and by version, shows how many sit on an older version, and lists the fields of a template with how many records fill them in, so you can see at a glance which questions nobody answers.
