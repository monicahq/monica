# Organise Knowledge in folders

Folders group the notes and lists of @doc(knowledge.introduction, "Knowledge") by theme. Chandler Bing keeps one for **Apartment**, one for **Work** and one for **Trips**. Folders sit side by side: a folder cannot hold another folder.

The column on the left of every Knowledge page lists your folders in the order you chose. Click a folder's name to fold or unfold it. The number beside it says how many notes and lists it holds.

## Add a folder

::::steps
:::step title="Open the menu"
Click the **+** button at the top of the column, next to **Knowledge**.

::screenshot{label="The Knowledge contents screen, with the folder tree on the left and the notes and lists of each folder" src="1-knowledge.index.webp"}
:::

:::step title="Choose New folder"
Choose **New folder**, type its name, then click **Add the folder**.
:::

:::step title="Find it at the end"
The new folder goes after the others. Move it where you want it, as described below.
:::
::::

The same menu offers **New note** and **New list**. It tells you which folder they go to: the folder of the note or list you are reading, or your first folder otherwise.

## Rename or reorder a folder

Open the folder's menu with the button at the end of its row:

- **Rename** turns the name into a field. Type the new name and press Enter. Double clicking the name does the same, and Escape leaves the name as it was.
- **Move above** and **Move below** move the folder one place up or down.

To move a note or a list to another folder, edit it and choose the folder there.

## Delete a folder

Choose **Delete folder** in the folder's menu. If the folder holds notes or lists, Monica asks what happens to them under **Its notes and lists**: move them to another folder, or delete them too.

:::warning
Choosing **Delete them too** deletes every note and list in the folder for good. Moving them keeps everything intact.
:::

Knowledge always keeps at least one folder, so your last folder cannot be deleted. Its menu says so instead of offering the deletion.
