# Relationships and life events

Monica records the people around you in two more ways than their pages. A relationship says how two people are connected: Ross is Emma’s **parent**, and Chandler’s **friend**. A life event says what happened, on which date and with whom: Monica and Chandler’s wedding, recorded once, with everybody who was there.

1. @doc(relationships.add). Say how somebody is connected to somebody else, even when you do not know the other person’s name.
2. @doc(relationships.edit). Record when a relationship started or ended, choose another type, or remove it.
3. @doc(life-events.record). Put something that happened on the timeline of everybody who took part.
4. @doc(life-events.timeline). Why one event appears on several pages, and in what order.

Both work on contacts. If you have nobody in Monica yet, start with @doc(contacts.add).
