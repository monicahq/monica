# Sign in with a passkey

A passkey signs you in with your phone, your computer or a security key, using your fingerprint, your face or the device’s own lock. There is no password to type or to steal. You need to have added one first, as @doc(security.passkeys) shows.

::::steps
:::step title="Start"
On the sign in screen, choose **Sign in with a passkey**.
:::

:::step title="Confirm on your device"
Your browser or device asks you to confirm, with your fingerprint, your face or its lock. Pick the passkey for Monica if it asks which one.
:::
::::

You are signed in, and Monica emails you with the subject **You signed in to Monica with a passkey**.

Monica asks for no two factor code after a passkey. A passkey already proves two things at once: that you hold the device, and that you can unlock it.

## When it does not work

- **No passkey button, or "Passkeys need a secure connection".** Passkeys work only over a secure connection, an address starting with `https`. Ask whoever runs your Monica.
- **Nothing was signed in. The prompt was dismissed.** You closed the device’s prompt. Choose **Sign in with a passkey** again.
- **That passkey cannot be used here.** The passkey belongs to another site or another account.
- **That did not work.** Try again, or sign in with your password or a link.

A browser or device that does not support passkeys does not show the button. Use another way in.
