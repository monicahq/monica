# Read notifications in the app

Everything Monica tells you in the app is kept in one list, behind the bell at the top of every screen. Use it to catch up on a reminder you dismissed, or one that arrived while you were away.

The **In the app** channel must be switched on for notifications to land here. @doc(notifications.channels) explains channels.

## Open your notifications

Choose the bell at the top right of any screen. A panel opens with your notifications, newest first. Each one shows what it is about, a line of detail, and when it arrived. The ones you have not read yet are marked with a dot, and the bell says how many there are.

When there is nothing new, the panel says **Nothing to catch up on**.

## Read one

Choose a notification. Monica marks it as read and takes you to what it is about, such as the reminder or the contact.

## Mark everything as read

Choose **Mark every one as read**, then **Yes, mark every one as read** to confirm. Monica says **Everything is marked as read**. Nothing is deleted: the notifications stay in the list, without their dot.

## How long notifications are kept

Monica keeps each notification in the app for 12 months by default, then removes it. Whoever runs your Monica can change that period.
