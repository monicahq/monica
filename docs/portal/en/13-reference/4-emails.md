# Emails Monica sends

Every email Monica can send you, by its subject. In the subjects, **Monica** is the name of your instance, and may read differently on one somebody else runs.

Each one is listed in **Emails sent** once sent, in your settings under **Logs**. See @doc(account.logs).

## Signing in and your account

| Subject | Sent when | Read next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirm your email address | You sign up, or change your email address | @doc(start.create-account) |
| Your sign-in link for Monica | You ask for a sign in link | @doc(signin.magic-link) |
| Choose a new password for Monica | You ask for a link to choose a new password | @doc(signin.forgot-password) |
| Failed sign-in attempt on your Monica account | Somebody types your address and a wrong password | @doc(security.suspicious-activity) |
| You signed in to Monica without a password | Somebody signs in with a link sent to you | @doc(security.suspicious-activity) |
| You signed in to Monica with a passkey | Somebody signs in with a passkey | @doc(security.suspicious-activity) |
| You signed in to Monica with Google | Somebody signs in through an outside service. The name of the service varies | @doc(security.suspicious-activity) |
| A sign-in from a new place on your Monica account | Somebody signs in from an internet address different from the last one | @doc(security.suspicious-activity) |
| A passkey was added to your Monica account | A passkey is added | @doc(security.passkeys) |
| A passkey was removed from your Monica account | A passkey is removed | @doc(security.passkeys) |
| A password was set on your Monica account | A password is set on an account that had none | @doc(security.password) |
| Your Monica password was changed | The password is changed, or reset from a link | @doc(security.password) |
| Your Monica password was removed | The password is removed | @doc(security.password) |
| Google was disconnected from your Monica account | An outside service is disconnected. The name of the service varies | @doc(signin.provider) |

## Reminders and notifications

| Subject | Sent when | Read next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The wording of your reminder | A reminder falls due, with the email channel on | @doc(reminders.answer) |
| You have 3 reminders today | Your daily summary, if you asked for one. The number varies | @doc(reminders.how-they-work) |
| This is what a notification from Monica looks like. | You choose **Send a test** on the **Email** channel | @doc(notifications.channels) |
| Telegram has been switched off | A channel fails several times in a row. The name of the channel varies | @doc(notifications.channels) |

The test of another channel, and the code that proves a phone number, arrive on that channel, not by email.

## Plans and billing, on monicahq.com

| Subject | Sent when |
| --- | --- |
| Your Monica account now has full access | A subscription starts, a purchase goes through, or full access is granted to you |
| Your Monica account is back on the free offer | A subscription ends, a payment fails several times, or a payment is refunded or disputed. Nothing is deleted |
