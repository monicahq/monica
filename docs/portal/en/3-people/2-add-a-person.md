# Add a person

Write somebody down in a vault, so that you have a page to keep what you know about them.

Say Phoebe has just married Mike Hannigan, and you want him in your **Personal** vault next to her. You add him once, and his page is ready for his birthday, his phone number and how he knows everybody else.

## Before you start

Open the vault the person belongs in. A contact stays in the vault you add them to, and Monica cannot move them to another vault afterwards. @doc(concepts.model) explains what a vault is for.

## Add the person

::::steps
:::step title="Open the form"
In the vault’s sidebar, choose **People**, then **Add somebody**. In a vault that holds nobody yet, the dashboard offers **Add the first person** instead.
:::

:::step title="Enter the name"
Type `Mike` in **First name** and `Hannigan` in **Last name**. Only the first name is required.

::screenshot{label="The New contact form, with the Name section, the Favourite and Partial contact switches, and the Create the contact button" src="1-contacts.new.webp"}
:::

:::step title="Add the rest of the name, if you know it"
Fill in **Prefix**, **Middle name**, **Suffix**, **Nickname** or **Name before marriage** when they apply. Leave the others empty.
:::

:::step title="Create the contact"
Choose **Create the contact**.
:::
::::

Monica says **Mike Hannigan is written down** and opens his page. The page is empty, ready for what you know about him. @doc(contacts.edit) shows how to fill it in.

## Choose how the name reads

**Name order** decides whether Monica writes the given name or the family name first, and sorts the person in your lists that way. Left on **Follow your own preference**, the given name comes first. Choose **Family name first** for somebody whose name is written that way, for example in Japanese or Hungarian. Below the field, **Written as** shows the name exactly as Monica will write it.

## Mark a favourite

Turn on **Favourite** for the people you look up most often. Favourites sit at the top of the list of people, under a heading of their own. You can change this later from the star on the person’s photo.

## Add a partial contact

Turn on **Partial contact** for somebody you want to name without keeping a full record about them, such as the barista at Central Perk whose last name you never learned.

A partial contact:

- has a page, and you can fill it in like any other;
- stays off the **Everybody** and **Favourites** lists, and appears only under **Partial contacts**;
- does not count toward the free offer on monicahq.com.

Most partial contacts are created for you when you link somebody to a person who is not in Monica yet, and leave **Create a contact record for this person** off. To turn a partial contact into a full one later, open the **Relationships** tab of the person they are linked to, choose **Manage**, then **Edit** on their row, and tick **Create a contact record for this person**.

## Add several people in a row

Tick **Create another one after this** before you choose **Create the contact**. Monica saves the person and opens an empty form again, instead of their page. This is quicker when you are writing down a whole family.

## Choose a template

A template decides which tabs and sections a contact’s page holds. Every vault has a default template for new contacts, and Monica uses it unless you pick another. When your account has more than one contact template, the form shows **What this contact starts from**, with a **Template** list to choose from. @doc(concepts.model) explains templates.

## If the form refuses

- **The vault has no template.** The form says **This vault has no template for new contacts to start from**. Right after you sign up, Monica is still preparing your templates: wait a moment and open the form again. Otherwise, give the vault a default template in its settings.
- **You reached the free offer.** On monicahq.com, the free offer holds a set number of contacts across all your vaults. When you reach it, the form says so and **Create the contact** stays unavailable. Full access lifts the limit. Partial contacts do not count toward it.

## After you save

Monica looks for possible duplicates in the background, for instance a second Mike Hannigan already in the vault. It never merges anybody on its own. To see what it found, open **People**, then the menu next to **Add somebody**, and choose **Manage duplicates**. @doc(duplicates.review) explains the list.
